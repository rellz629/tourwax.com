## Writing Guidelines

- **Author**: "TourWax Team"
- **Tone**: Informative, enthusiastic but not hype-y. Helpful and practical.
- **Length**: 800-1500 words per post
- **Internal links**: Every post should link to at least 3 internal pages (artist, city, genre, venue, or other blog posts)
- **SEO**: Include target keyword in title, first paragraph, and 1-2 subheadings
- **Frontmatter format**:
  ```yaml
  ---
  title: "Post Title"
  slug: "post-slug"
  excerpt: "1-2 sentence summary for listings and meta descriptions"
  author: "TourWax Team"
  category: "Guide"
  featuredImage: null
  publishedAt: "2026-04-01"
  updatedAt: "2026-04-01"
  ---
  ```
