2026 is shaping up to be one of the biggest years for live music in recent memory. After years of pent-up demand and record-breaking tours, artists across every genre are hitting the road with ambitious new productions. Here are the tours we're most excited about this year.

## Stadium Tours Leading the Way

The stadium circuit is packed this year. Major pop and rock acts are bringing their biggest productions yet to cities across the country. If you haven't already checked for shows in your area, now's the time. Many of these runs are selling out fast, with secondary market prices climbing as dates approach.

Keep an eye on the [Artists page](/artists) for the latest tour date announcements. New shows are being added regularly as demand grows.

## Hip-Hop and R&B on the Road

The hip-hop and R&B touring scene continues to grow. Arena-level rappers are investing more in stage production and setlists, making their live shows a must-see experience. Many of these tours are hitting major markets like [New York](/concerts/new-york), [Los Angeles](/concerts/los-angeles), [Atlanta](/concerts/atlanta), and [Houston](/concerts/houston).

Collaborative tours featuring multiple artists on the same bill are especially popular this year, giving fans incredible value with several headliner-level performances in a single night.

## Rock and Alternative Reunions

2026 has brought some long-awaited reunion tours. Bands that haven't toured together in years are getting back on the road, and fans are responding with overwhelming demand. These nostalgia-driven tours tend to sell out quickly, so don't wait if one of your favorites announces dates.

Check out our [Rock tours page](/tours/rock) for the latest in guitar-driven live music.

## Country Music's Growing Footprint

Country music touring has expanded dramatically. What was once a genre largely confined to certain markets is now filling arenas and stadiums in cities nationwide. New-generation country artists are blending traditional sounds with pop and rock influences, drawing in broader audiences.

From stadium shows to amphitheater circuits, country tours are among the best-selling in 2026. Browse [Country tours](/tours/country) to find shows near you.

## Electronic and Festival Season

Festival season is bigger than ever in 2026, with electronic artists headlining major festivals and launching their own solo tours. Production value in electronic music live shows continues to push boundaries with immersive visuals and state-of-the-art sound systems.

Many electronic artists follow up their festival appearances with club and theater tours in the fall, offering a more intimate experience. Check the [Electronic tours page](/tours/electronic) for upcoming dates.

## How to Stay on Top of Tour Announcements

With so much happening, it's easy to miss an announcement. Here's how to keep up:

- **Browse TourWax regularly.** We track tour dates from multiple sources and update our listings throughout the day.
- **Check by city.** Visit our [Concerts page](/concerts) to see everything happening in your area, across all genres and venues.
- **Explore by genre.** Our [Tours page](/tours) breaks down upcoming shows by genre so you can find exactly what you're looking for.

2026 is a year you don't want to spend on the sidelines. Get out and see some live music.
