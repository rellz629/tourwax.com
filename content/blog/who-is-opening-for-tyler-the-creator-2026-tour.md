[Tyler, The Creator](/artists/tyler-the-creator) brought one of the most talked-about stadium shows of the touring cycle with the Chromakopia World Tour, and the support lineup is exactly what you'd expect from an artist who treats the entire show as a singular creative statement. Teezo Touchdown and Paris Texas are the names you need to know before you walk in.

## The Opening Acts

### Teezo Touchdown

Teezo Touchdown is the main direct support act on the North American leg. If you're not familiar, fix that before the show. The Houston-raised artist makes genre-resistant music that sits somewhere between punk, R&B, and hip-hop, delivered with a live energy that reads more like a rock performance than anything from a conventional rap show. He released his debut album *How Do You Sleep at Night?* to serious critical acclaim and has since built a reputation as one of the most compelling live performers of his generation.

Teezo's stage presence is especially relevant here because he's not just warming up the crowd. He's a legitimate headliner in his own right, and watching him perform in a support slot is the kind of thing you'll recount to people years later. Tyler brought him on precisely because the contrast of styles makes both sets hit harder.

### Paris Texas

The Los Angeles rap duo handles opener duties on select dates. Louie and Bobby Paris have been building serious momentum since their *Boy Anonymous* project drew comparisons to early Odd Future energy, which makes the pairing with Tyler feel like a deliberate full-circle statement. Their set is aggressive, playful, and short in the best way: a concentrated blast of energy designed to prime a stadium crowd.

## What Makes This Tour Different

Tyler's approach to touring has always been more theatrical than most. The Chromakopia World Tour is built around the album's visual world: the wooden mask, the muted earth tones, the sense of introspection filtered through maximalist production. The staging is designed to feel less like a concert and more like an immersive performance, which means the opener role is about tone-setting as much as hype-building.

Both Teezo and Paris Texas understand this. Their sets function as acts in a larger show rather than standalone openers killing time before the main event.

## About the Chromakopia Album

*Chromakopia* arrived in October 2024 as Tyler's seventh studio album, a meditation on identity, legacy, and growth delivered through a sonic palette that pulled from gospel, jazz, and classic hip-hop. It debuted at number one globally and generated some of the strongest critical response of his career. The tour is built to match its scale: arenas and stadiums, with production that matches the album's cinematic ambition.
