## The Tour by the Numbers

The Still the Problem Tour opened April 10, 2026 at U.S. Bank Stadium in Minneapolis and covered 11 cities across 22 stadium dates, supporting Wallen's 2025 album *I'm the Problem*. The route included NFL stadiums in Las Vegas, Denver, Pittsburgh, Chicago, Baltimore, and Philadelphia, plus three college football cathedrals:

- **Saban Field at Bryant-Denny Stadium** in Tuscaloosa, home of Alabama football
- **Ben Hill Griffin Stadium** in Gainesville, "The Swamp"
- **Michigan Stadium** in Ann Arbor, the largest stadium in the United States

Rather than a fixed opening act, Wallen ran a rotating support card all tour: Brooks & Dunn and [Thomas Rhett](/artists/thomas-rhett) anchored most Night 1 shows, while Ella Langley and [HARDY](/artists/hardy) headlined support on Night 2 dates. Gavin Adcock and Hudson Westbrook split the mid-card slot across the run.
