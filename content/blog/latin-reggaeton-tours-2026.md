## Why Latin Touring Is Exploding

A few years ago, a Latin artist playing a baseball stadium in the U.S. would have been headline news. In 2026, Fuerza Regida is doing it in eight cities. The shift is driven by several factors:

- **Streaming crossover:** Artists like Bad Bunny and Peso Pluma regularly top the global Spotify charts, building fanbases that cross language barriers
- **Musica mexicana's rise:** Regional Mexican music has gone from niche to mainstream, with artists like Junior H, Natanael Cano, and Fuerza Regida leading the charge
- **Demand in key markets:** Cities like [Houston](/concerts/houston), [Los Angeles](/concerts/los-angeles), [Dallas](/concerts/dallas), [Chicago](/concerts/chicago), and [Miami](/concerts/miami) have massive Latin music audiences that can fill venues multiple nights in a row
- **Festival presence:** Latin artists are increasingly headlining major festivals, exposing new audiences to the genre
