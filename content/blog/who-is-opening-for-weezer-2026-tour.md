[Weezer](/artists/weezer) is taking over arenas this fall with **The Gathering**, a North American tour running from September 9 in Sacramento through October 25 in Los Angeles. The support bill is the same every night, and it's a good one: **The Shins and Silversun Pickups open all dates.** Three bands, three distinct eras of alternative rock, one ticket.

## The Openers

### The Shins

James Mercer's indie-pop institution is the direct support act. If you were anywhere near a college radio station in the 2000s, "New Slang" and "Caring Is Creepy" are permanent fixtures in your brain (thanks in part to *Garden State*), and "Phantom Limb" and "Simple Song" filled out one of the most beloved catalogs of the era. The Shins don't tour constantly, so getting them on an arena run is a legitimate draw in its own right.

### Silversun Pickups

The Los Angeles quartet built one of the most recognizable sounds of late-2000s alternative: swirling, distorted, and anchored by "Lazy Eye," "Panic Switch," and "The Royal We." Their swelling wall-of-sound style translates naturally to arena rooms, and they've stayed a consistent live act for two decades.

## Why This Bill Works

This is a nostalgia-forward package done right. Weezer's *Blue Album* and *Pinkerton* material sits next to The Shins' 2000s indie canon and Silversun Pickups' alt-radio era, which means the audience skews from Gen X through elder millennial, and every band on the bill has at least a few songs the entire room knows. It follows the same playbook as Weezer's past package tours with Pixies and Green Day: stack the bill so the openers alone are worth the ticket.

Expect Weezer's set to lean on the classics: "Buddy Holly," "Say It Ain't So," "Island in the Sun," "Hash Pipe," "Beverly Hills," plus whatever deep cuts Rivers Cuomo is in the mood for. The band has a long history of surprise covers, and arena tours tend to bring out the crowd-pleaser version of Weezer.

## Ticket Tips

1. **Weeknight shows are the value play.** Tuesday and Wednesday dates in mid-size markets consistently price below the weekend coastal shows.
2. **All three bands play full-length opener sets.** Doors-to-Weezer takes a while; showing up late means missing half of what you paid for.
3. **Check resale the week of.** Alternative-rock package tours historically see prices drop close to show day in all but the biggest markets.
