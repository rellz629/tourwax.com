Yes, Haircut 100 are touring in 2026, and it is their busiest year in four decades. The reunited new wave band, led by Nick Heyward, spends August and September on a North American run with Squeeze and Adam Ant, plays a rare headline club show in Los Angeles, and closes the year supporting Culture Club on a UK arena tour in December. ## The Comeback, Briefly

Haircut 100 split in the mid-80s after *Pelican West* made them one of the defining acts of the new wave era. The classic lineup reunited in 2023 for a sold-out show at London's Shepherd's Bush Empire, followed by UK and US dates and a Glastonbury appearance. *Boxing The Compass*, released June 19, 2026, is their first album of new material since the reunion, and this tour cycle is the biggest they have mounted since the original run.
