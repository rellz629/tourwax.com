Logic and G-Eazy are taking the Endless Summer Tour back out for Part II. The co-headlining run picks up where their original 2018 outing left off, with 23 amphitheater dates across the U.S. and Canada. The tour kicks off Tuesday, September 15 in Kansas City, MO and closes out Wednesday, October 28 at Shoreline Amphitheatre in Mountain View, CA.

## What to Expect

The original Endless Summer Tour in 2018 paired Logic and G-Eazy at a peak moment for both: Logic was riding "1-800-273-8255" and the YSIV album cycle, while G-Eazy was off The Beautiful & Damned. Part II reunites the two for a victory-lap run aimed squarely at fans who came up on that era of streaming-era hip-hop.

Expect a setlist heavy on hits from both catalogs: Logic's "Homicide," "1-800-273-8255," "Everybody," and tracks from his more recent College Park era, alongside G-Eazy staples like "Me, Myself & I," "No Limit," and "I Mean It." The amphitheater-heavy routing (Merriweather, PNC Bank Arts Center, Pine Knob, Shoreline) leans into the tour's late-summer-into-fall vibe.

Presales have already kicked off, with general onsale rolling through April and May.
