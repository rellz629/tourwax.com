_Updated July 25, 2026: the European stadium leg wrapped July 5 in London. What's left in 2026 is the Las Vegas Sphere residency (no openers) starting October 1 and the two Mohegan Sun shows in November with Suicidal Tendencies and Spiritbox._

[Metallica](/artists/metallica) has been on the road all year for another chapter of the M72 World Tour, and the support lineup is exactly the kind of stacked metal bill the band has built the M72 era around. The European stadium leg paired Pantera with Avatar on one night and Gojira with Knocked Loose on the other, with Metallica playing a different setlist each evening. Suicidal Tendencies and Spiritbox split the two-night Mohegan Sun stand this November. The Las Vegas Sphere residency is a no-opener immersive run. Here's exactly who you'll see and on which night.

## The M72 Two-Night Format

If you're new to the M72 era, here's the trick: when Metallica plays a city for two nights, they bring different openers and a different setlist for each show. No song is repeated across the two nights. That's why a "two-day pass" exists for almost every M72 city, and why dedicated fans buy both. The 2026 European leg keeps that structure intact.

Two pairings of openers rotate across the European leg:

- **Night One: Pantera + Avatar**
- **Night Two: Gojira + Knocked Loose**

In single-night cities, it's still one of those two pairings. Below is the full breakdown.

### Pantera: The M72 Era Headliner-Sized Opener

Pantera (in their post-2022 reunion lineup with Phil Anselmo, Rex Brown, Zakk Wylde, and Charlie Benante) returns to the M72 stage for another run after their 2023-2024 dates with Metallica. They are arguably the highest-profile support act in modern Metallica history; this is a band that headlined Wacken and arenas in their own right. On 2026 dates they share the bill with Swedish theatrical metallers Avatar.

### Gojira: French Progressive Metal Heavyweights

Gojira have spent the last decade ascending from cult favorites to genuine festival headliners, and their 2024 Olympics opening ceremony performance only widened that audience. They return as M72 night-two support, paired with Knocked Loose.

### Knocked Loose: Hardcore's Crossover Moment

The Kentucky hardcore band has been on one of the most explosive trajectories in heavy music: a Coachella Sahara tent slot in 2024, a Grammy nomination, and now opening Metallica stadiums in Europe. Their inclusion is a signal of the M72 booking philosophy — Metallica is using these slots to platform the heaviest bands working in 2026.

### Avatar: Theatrical Swedish Metal

Avatar's circus-meets-metal stagecraft makes them a natural fit alongside Pantera, providing visual contrast to Pantera's straight-ahead groove metal attack. Expect face paint, ringmaster narration, and one of the more visually distinctive opening sets in metal.

## Mohegan Sun: Suicidal Tendencies and Spiritbox

The two-night Mohegan Sun Arena run in Uncasville, Connecticut (November 19-22) brings a different set of openers, with each night featuring a single support band rather than the European two-band format:

- **November 20** — Suicidal Tendencies
- **November 22** — Spiritbox

Mike Muir's Suicidal Tendencies represents the L.A. crossover thrash/hardcore scene that birthed Robert Trujillo (S.T. is essentially Trujillo's alma mater). Spiritbox, the Vancouver-based progressive metalcore breakout, gives the second night a more modern, post-djent sound. The pairing is a smart microcosm of M72's "old school plus new school" approach.

## Sphere Las Vegas Residency: No Opener, Two Setlists

Metallica's Sphere Las Vegas residency is a different beast entirely. It opens October 1, 2026, runs weekly pairs through November 8, and picks back up with a second block from late January through mid-March 2027. There's no opening act. Sphere shows are immersive headlining residencies, with the band taking the full evening to leverage the venue's wraparound 16K LED screen and spatial audio. The Sphere run alternates between two distinct setlists:

- **"Metallica"** — the standard M72 set
- **"Metallica: Life Burns Faster"** — a separate setlist that emphasizes different eras of the catalog, with bespoke Sphere visuals

Like the European leg, the residency rewards two-night attendance: the experience is built around seeing both setlists. Two-day passes are sold for each weekly pair.

## Why This Tour Matters

The M72 World Tour has now stretched across four years (2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026), making it the longest-running tour cycle of Metallica's career. The 2026 leg is essentially the victory lap, and the booking decisions reflect that. There's no need to bet on unknown openers anymore. Pantera, Gojira, Knocked Loose, and Avatar are all the right size and pedigree to play stadiums on their own merits.

The Sphere residency is the splash. Metallica becomes the first metal band to take a full residency at the venue, and the two-setlist format gives die-hards a real reason to make multiple trips to Vegas. That kind of commitment from an artist of Metallica's stature is rare; the last time they did anything similar was the Lou Reed-era weirdness or the WorldWired Tour stops at S&M2.

For European fans, the two-night format remains the M72 calling card. If you're going to one show, pick your night based on the openers. If you're going to two, you're getting four bands and zero repeat songs from Metallica.
