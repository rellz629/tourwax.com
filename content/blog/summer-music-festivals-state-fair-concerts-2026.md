Summer 2026 is shaping up to be one of the biggest festival seasons in years. From state fair concert series to multi-day music festivals, there's something for every genre and budget. Here's what you need to know.

## State Fair Concerts 2026

State fairs remain one of the best ways to see major artists at affordable prices. Many fairs include concert admission with your gate ticket, making them some of the best deals in live music.

### Fairs to Watch

- **[California Mid-State Fair](/venues/california-mid-state-fair):** Paso Robles, CA hosts one of the West Coast's premier fair concert series every July. Past headliners have included country, rock, and Latin artists. Check the [venue page](/venues/california-mid-state-fair) for 2026 lineup announcements.
- **[Delaware State Fair](/venues/delaware-state-fair):** Harrington, DE brings national touring acts to the Eastern Shore each July. Browse [upcoming shows](/venues/delaware-state-fair).
- **[Washington State Fair](/venues/washington-state-fair):** Puyallup, WA is one of the largest fairs in the Pacific Northwest, with a concert lineup that typically spans country, rock, and pop.

State fair concerts are announced on a rolling basis through spring and early summer. Bookmark our [festivals page](/festivals) to see lineups as they're announced.

## Multi-Day Music Festivals

Multi-day festivals offer the chance to see dozens of artists across multiple stages. These events often sell out well in advance, so grab tickets early.

### Rock & Alternative

- **[Reading Festival](/festivals/reading-festival-2026-friday-2026-08-28):** The UK's legendary Reading Festival returns August 28-30 with a stacked lineup across multiple stages. Check the [Friday lineup](/festivals/reading-festival-2026-friday-2026-08-28) for confirmed acts.

### Country & Americana

- **[Willie Nelson's Outlaw Music Festival](/artists/willie-nelson):** A 12-date touring festival hitting Texas, the Midwest, and Northeast from July through August. Featuring The Avett Brothers, Sheryl Crow, Wilco, and more. [See all dates](/artists/willie-nelson).

### Hip-Hop & R&B

- **[Tycoon Music Festival](/festivals/tycoon-music-festival-2026-04-04):** Atlanta's hip-hop festival brings together top R&B and hip-hop acts for a stacked single-day event.

### Multi-Genre

- **[Parkfest 2026](/festivals/parkfest-2026-2026-04-25):** One of the season's first major festivals, Parkfest features a diverse lineup spanning multiple genres. [See the full lineup](/festivals/parkfest-2026-2026-04-25).

## How to Find Festival Tickets

Ticket prices for summer festivals vary widely. Here are some tips:

1. **Buy early.** Most festivals offer tiered pricing. Early bird tickets can save you 30-50% compared to gate prices.
2. **Compare prices.** Use [TourWax](/festivals) to compare ticket prices from Ticketmaster and SeatGeek for the same event.
3. **Check state fair bundles.** Many state fairs include concert admission with your fair ticket, making them incredible value.
4. **Watch for lineup additions.** Festivals often announce headliners in waves. Subscribe to artist pages on TourWax to get notified when new dates are added.
