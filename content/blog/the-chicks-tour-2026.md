The Chicks has announced 9 tour dates as part of the 'Tim McGraw with 49 Winchester, The Chicks, Lady A and more' tour. The run kicks off Saturday, June 13 in Highland, CA and wraps up Sunday, August 23 in Minneapolis, MN.

## Tour Dates

### June 2026
- **Saturday, June 13** — Yaamava Theater at Yaamava Resort and Casino, Highland, CA
- **Sunday, June 14** — The Venue at Thunder Valley Casino Resort, Lincoln, CA
- **Saturday, June 20** — Missoula Fairgrounds, Missoula, MT

### July 2026
- **Saturday, July 11** — Hersheypark Stadium, Hershey, PA
- **Thursday, July 30** — Fenway Park, Boston, MA

### August 2026
- **Saturday, August 1** — Hard Rock Live at Etess Arena, Atlantic City, NJ
- **Friday, August 7** — Burl's Creek Event Grounds, Oro-Medonte, ON
- **Sunday, August 23** — Illinois State Fairgrounds, Springfield, IL
- **Sunday, August 23** — Target Field, Minneapolis, MN
