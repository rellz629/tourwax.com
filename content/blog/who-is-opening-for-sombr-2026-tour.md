[sombr](/artists/sombr) is taking the You Are the Reason Tour across North America from July through November 2026, his first arena headline run after a breakout year. The support bill is one of the most varied of any tour this fall: legacy post-punk, baroque pop, a TikTok balladeer, and a Disney-pop star all take turns warming up the room. Here's who's opening each leg and what to expect when the doors open.

## How the Lineup Works

The 20-year-old singer, born Shane Boose and raised on the Lower East Side of New York, broke through in 2025 with his debut album *I Barely Know Her* and the singles "Back to Friends" and "Undressed," both of which reached the top 20 of the Billboard Hot 100. After a Coachella 2026 debut where he performed "1979" alongside Smashing Pumpkins frontman Billy Corgan, he announced a full arena tour for the back half of the year.

Rather than carry one opener for the whole run, the booking pairs him with six different acts who each take a defined stretch of the calendar. The summer warm-up dates ride with one opener, the fall arena leg hands off from city cluster to city cluster, and a couple of additional supporting artists fill in on select nights. The result is a tour that feels noticeably different depending on which leg you catch.

## The Opening Acts

### King Princess

King Princess opens the two summer warm-up dates: Mexico City on July 22 and the pair of Red Rocks shows on July 26 and 27. The New York singer-songwriter, born Mikaela Straus, broke out in 2018 with "1950" on Mark Ronson's Zelig Records and has released two albums, *Cheap Queen* and *Hold On Baby*. Her glam-tinged indie pop and easy stage presence make the Red Rocks sunsets a strong opening note for the tour.

### Interpol

Interpol kick off the fall arena leg in Vancouver on September 29. The pairing is the most surprising on the bill: the New York post-punk band formed in 1997 and their 2002 debut *Turn on the Bright Lights* is a cornerstone of the early-2000s revival. Bringing a veteran act on board signals how much of sombr's sound is rooted in moody, guitar-forward atmospherics rather than straight pop.

### The Hellp

The Hellp take the Pacific Northwest dates, Seattle on October 1 and Portland on October 2. The Los Angeles duo make abrasive, internet-native electronic rock and have built a devoted online following. They bring the loudest, most chaotic energy of any opener on the run, which makes the two-night Northwest stretch the outlier on the calendar.

### Tom Odell

Tom Odell supports the California swing, from Sacramento on October 6 through San Diego on October 13. The English singer-songwriter won the BRIT Critics' Choice Award in 2013, and his 2012 single "Another Love" found a massive second life on TikTok years after release, racking up billions of streams. His piano-driven balladry is the most direct tonal match for sombr's own slow-burning songwriting.

### The Last Dinner Party

The Last Dinner Party handle the longest stretch of the tour, from Glendale on October 14 through Chicago on November 4. The London band won the BRITs Rising Star Award in 2024 and the BBC Sound of 2024 poll, and their debut *Prelude to Ecstasy* hit number one on the UK Albums Chart in February 2024 with the highest first-week sales for a debut album in nearly a decade. Their theatrical, baroque-pop staging gives the Southern and Midwest dates the most dramatic opening set of the run.

### Dove Cameron

Dove Cameron closes out the tour, from Indianapolis on November 6 through the two hometown Madison Square Garden shows on November 23 and 24. The actor-turned-pop-artist broke through on Disney Channel before her 2022 single "Boyfriend" went platinum and earned a Grammy nomination. Her dark, synth-leaning pop bridges the gap between sombr's alt sensibility and a mainstream arena crowd, a fitting note to ride into the New York finale.

A pair of additional acts fill in on select dates: New Zealand alt-pop band Balu Brigada joins the early Pacific Northwest run, and Sub Pop indie artist Hannah Jadagu appears on select dates in the back half of the tour.

## What to Expect

You Are the Reason is sombr's jump from clubs and theaters straight into arenas, a fast climb built on the streaming numbers behind *I Barely Know Her*. The opener rotation is the clearest signal of how he's positioning the era: Interpol and Tom Odell lend critical and catalog weight, The Last Dinner Party bring the most theatrical staging, The Hellp bring volume and chaos, and King Princess and Dove Cameron keep the bill anchored in melodic pop. Catching the tour on two different legs would genuinely deliver two different opening experiences.

The two-night Madison Square Garden finale on November 23 and 24 is the centerpiece of the run, a hometown closer for an artist who was playing far smaller New York rooms barely a year earlier.
