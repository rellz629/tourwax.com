## Red Rocks Basics: What to Know Before You Go

**Where it is.** Red Rocks Amphitheatre sits in Morrison, Colorado, about 15 miles west of [Denver](/concerts/denver). Most concertgoers stay in Denver or Golden and drive or shuttle in.

**Tickets.** Primary tickets for Red Rocks shows sell through AXS rather than Ticketmaster for most events, with resale available on SeatGeek and other platforms. Artist pages on TourWax link to current listings for tracked shows.

**The altitude is real.** The amphitheatre sits at roughly 6,450 feet, and your seats are up a serious set of stairs. Hydrate before and during the show, pace yourself on alcohol, and give yourself extra time to climb if you're coming from sea level.

**Weather changes fast.** Evening shows can swing 30 degrees from gates to encore, and fall shows regularly dip below freezing after dark. Layers are non-negotiable for September through November dates. Shows go on rain or shine, and umbrellas aren't allowed, so pack a poncho.

**Get there early.** Parking lots open early and fill up, and the walk from the lots to the seating bowl is a hike in itself. Arriving at least 90 minutes before showtime is the standard advice.

Roughly 70 concerts remain on the calendar from August through mid-November 2026, including multi-night runs from Goose, LCD Soundsystem, GRiZ, Brandi Carlile, Andrea Bocelli, Zac Brown Band, and deadmau5.

### When does the Red Rocks 2026 season end?
The last currently announced shows are mike. on November 14-15, 2026. The venue occasionally adds late-season dates, and the 2027 season typically begins announcing over the winter.

### Was Rezz Rocks 2026 canceled?
Yes. Rezz Rocks VIII, scheduled for October 3, 2026, was canceled. Deorro now plays that date, and refunds for the canceled show process automatically through your point of purchase.

### What is the capacity of Red Rocks Amphitheatre?
Red Rocks holds 9,525 people. It's a naturally formed, open-air amphitheatre with long bench rows rather than individual seats, and many shows sell general admission, so arrival time matters more than at a typical arena.
