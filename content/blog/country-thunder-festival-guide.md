Country Thunder is one of the biggest country festival brands in North America, with editions in Saskatchewan, Alberta, Arizona, Wisconsin, and Florida. In 2026 the story split in two directions: the Saskatchewan festival in Craven delivered one of its strongest lineups ever, while the Calgary edition was cancelled outright.

Here is the honest rundown of where every Country Thunder stands, what happened in 2026, and what to watch for in 2027.

## Country Thunder Saskatchewan 2026: The Craven Recap

Country Thunder Saskatchewan, held in the Qu'Appelle Valley at Craven (the site long known for the Craven Country Jamboree), ran **July 9 to 12, 2026** with headliners:

- **[Lainey Wilson](/artists/lainey-wilson)**
- **[Creed](/artists/creed)** crossing over from the rock world for one of the weekend's most talked-about sets
- **[Riley Green](/artists/riley-green)**

The undercard was deep too: Gavin Adcock, The Dead South, Robyn Ottolini, [Cameron Whitcomb](/artists/cameron-whitcomb), [Dasha](/artists/dasha), [Max McNown](/artists/max-mcnown), Noeline Hofmann, Logan Layman, and more filled out four days of camping and country in the valley.

Craven's festival is one of Canada's largest country music events, with tens of thousands of campers turning the valley into a temporary city each July.

## Country Thunder Alberta (Calgary) 2026: Cancelled

If you searched for a Country Thunder Calgary 2026 lineup and found nothing, there is a reason: **Country Thunder Alberta 2026 did not happen.** Organizers cancelled the Calgary edition, citing conditions created by the City of Calgary, including active construction around the festival site and new sound restrictions.

As of August 2026, the official Country Thunder site lists Alberta's status as "check back for 2027 dates." No 2027 dates or lineup have been announced for Calgary yet. We will update this guide if the Alberta edition confirms its return.

## The Other Country Thunder Festivals

Country Thunder also runs editions in the United States:

- **Country Thunder Arizona** in Florence, Arizona, typically held in April
- **Country Thunder Wisconsin** in Twin Lakes, Wisconsin, typically held in July
- **Country Thunder Florida**, the newest edition

Lineups for the following year are usually announced in late fall. ## FAQ

### Did Country Thunder Calgary happen in 2026?

No. Country Thunder Alberta 2026 was cancelled due to conditions around the Calgary festival site, including construction and new sound limitations. No 2027 dates have been announced yet.

### Who headlined Country Thunder Saskatchewan 2026?

Lainey Wilson, Creed, and Riley Green headlined the festival in Craven, Saskatchewan, July 9 to 12, 2026, supported by Gavin Adcock, The Dead South, Dasha, Max McNown, and more.

### Where is Country Thunder Saskatchewan held?

At the longtime festival grounds in Craven, Saskatchewan, in the Qu'Appelle Valley about 30 minutes north of Regina. The site hosted the Craven Country Jamboree for decades before joining the Country Thunder family.

### When do Country Thunder tickets go on sale?

Each edition announces its own on-sale, typically in late fall when the next year's lineup is revealed. Camping packages tend to sell out fastest.
