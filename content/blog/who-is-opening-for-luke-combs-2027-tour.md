Four acts open for [Luke Combs](/artists/luke-combs) on the 2027 leg of the My Kinda Saturday Night Tour: **Treaty Oak Revival, Avery Anna, Shenandoah, and Wyatt McCubbin**. Unlike the 2026 run, where the support bill rotated city by city, all four openers are billed on all 12 dates. Whichever stadium you pick, you get the same four sets.

Combs announced the 2027 extension on August 13, 2026. It is a 12-show stadium run staged in the round, opening April 3, 2027 at AT&T Stadium in Arlington and closing June 26, 2027 at Petco Park in San Diego. Tickets went on general sale Friday, August 21, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time.

Two gaps in that routing are worth noting. There is no show on May 22, so the Kansas City and Edmonton dates sit two weeks apart, and the tour crosses into Canada for back-to-back weekends in Edmonton and Vancouver before turning south. Boise is the outlier booking here: Albertsons Stadium is a college football stadium on the Boise State campus, and it is the smallest venue on the run by a wide margin.

## Who These Openers Actually Are

### Treaty Oak Revival

The heaviest thing on the bill. Treaty Oak Revival formed in Odessa, Texas in 2018 and singer Sam Canty has described them as "a rock band with a country accent," which undersells how loud they are live. Their 2025 album *West Texas Degenerate* hit No. 21 on the Billboard 200 and No. 1 on Top Rock Albums, and "Bad State of Mind" carried them onto the rock charts proper. If you have only heard the Combs radio singles, this is the set that will surprise you.

### Avery Anna

The newest name of the four in radio terms. Anna is from Flagstaff, Arizona, was named ACM New Female Artist of the Year, and released *Breakup Over Breakfast* in 2024 and *Let Go Letters* in 2025. She co-wrote and features on Sam Barber's "Indigo," which reached the top 10 on Hot Country Songs, and she made her country radio debut with "Blood Runs Thicker" in July 2026.

### Shenandoah

The reason a lot of parents will be in the building early. The Muscle Shoals, Alabama band has been running since the mid-1980s, and Marty Raybon is back out front alongside founding drummer Mike McGuire after rejoining the group. Expect "Two Dozen Roses," "The Church on Cumberland Road," and "Somewhere in the Vicinity of the Heart." The band spent 2026 on a 40th anniversary run, so this bill lands them in front of stadium crowds at exactly the right moment.

### Wyatt McCubbin

The straight-ahead traditionalist slot. McCubbin has spent years as a Nashville songwriter with cuts by Tracy Lawrence, Zach Top, Josh Ward, Jacob Bryant, and Jake Worthington. He signed his first record deal in 2026 with MCA/Lucille and cut his self-titled debut album with producer Dave Cobb. This tour is by far the biggest stage he has played.

## How 2027 Differs From the 2026 Run

The 2026 leg was the bigger operation on paper: a North American stadium run that opened March 21, 2026 at Allegiant Stadium outside Las Vegas, then a European leg from July 4 through August 2 that finished with three nights at Wembley Stadium in London. That leg carried a rotating opener bill built around Dierks Bentley in North America and Thomas Rhett and The Script in Europe. If you are looking for that schedule, we have it in [who is opening for Luke Combs in 2026](/blog/who-is-opening-for-luke-combs-2026-tour).

The 2027 leg is a different shape. Twelve shows, one weekly cadence, one fixed support bill, and a stage in the round for every date. In the round means there is no true back of the house, which changes how you should think about seat selection: the cheap upper corners behind the stage are no longer dead zones, because there is no behind the stage.

Both legs support *The Way I Am*.

## FAQ

### Who is opening for Luke Combs in 2027?

Treaty Oak Revival, Avery Anna, Shenandoah, and Wyatt McCubbin. All four are billed on all 12 dates of the 2027 My Kinda Saturday Night Tour.

### Is the opener different at each show?

No. This is the rare stadium tour with a fixed support bill. Every one of the 12 dates has the same four openers, which is a change from the 2026 leg, where the lineup rotated by city and by continent.

### When does the 2027 Luke Combs tour start and end?

It opens Saturday, April 3, 2027 at [AT&T Stadium](/venues/att-stadium) in Arlington, Texas and closes Saturday, June 26, 2027 at [Petco Park](/venues/petco-park) in San Diego.

### Are all the 2027 dates on Saturdays?

Yes. All 12 shows fall on a Saturday, in keeping with the My Kinda Saturday Night name. There is one skipped weekend, May 22, between Kansas City and Edmonton.

### Is Dierks Bentley opening again in 2027?

No. Dierks Bentley supported the 2026 North American leg. He is not billed on any 2027 date.

### Which Canadian cities are on the 2027 tour?

Two: Edmonton at Commonwealth Stadium on May 29 and Vancouver at [BC Place](/venues/bc-place) on June 5.

### When do tickets go on sale?

General on-sale opened Friday, August 21, 2026 at 10 a.m. local venue time through Ticketmaster, after fan club and venue presales that ran August 18 through August 20. Remaining inventory and resale listings are live now.
