Noah Kahan is bringing The Great Divide Tour to some of the most storied venues in North America this summer: five nights at Fenway Park, two at Wrigley Field, the Rose Bowl, Citi Field, and a closer at T-Mobile Park in Seattle. It's a landmark run for a folk singer from Strafford, Vermont. Here's who's opening and the full schedule.

## The Opening Acts

Two singer-songwriters join [Noah Kahan](/artists/noah-kahan) on all North American headlining dates: Gigi Perez and Annabelle Dinda. Both are indie folk and acoustic artists whose styles fit naturally into the evening's tone.

### Gigi Perez

Gigi Perez grew up in West Palm Beach, Florida, started uploading songs to TikTok in 2021 while coping with the death of her older sister, and went viral almost immediately. Her song "Celene" spread across the platform before she had a label deal, and she eventually signed with Island Records. In July 2024, she released "Sailor Song," an acoustic queer ballad that reached number 22 on the Billboard Hot 100 and turned her from a TikTok artist into a proper charting one.

Her debut album, *At the Beach, in Every Life*, followed in April 2025. The record leans into spare, emotionally direct songwriting that should work well for a crowd arriving in a ballpark at dusk.

### Annabelle Dinda

Annabelle Dinda is a New York-based singer-songwriter who graduated from NYU's Gallatin School and has been releasing music quietly since 2020. Her song "The Hand" went viral on TikTok in late 2025, leading into her 2026 album *Some Things Never Leave*, produced by Jacob Portrait of Unknown Mortal Orchestra. The album gave her a breakthrough moment right as this tour was being assembled. Her alt-folk sound sits in the same territory as Kahan's, which makes her a logical first act for this particular audience.

## Festival Appearance

Kahan also plays **Bonnaroo Music Festival** on June 14 in Manchester, Tennessee. Festival lineups operate separately from the headlining tour dates, so the opener situation at Bonnaroo is different.

## The Five Fenway Nights

The Boston stretch is the centerpiece of this tour. Five consecutive nights at Fenway Park across July 7, 8, 10, 11, and 12 is an extraordinary run for any artist. Kahan sold out Fenway on a previous visit within minutes, and the demand for this run tells the same story. For fans in the Northeast who have been waiting for Kahan to play a venue close to Vermont, this is the moment.

## International Leg

After the North American run closes in Seattle on August 30, the tour moves to Europe, Australia, and New Zealand through December 2026. Those international dates feature different support acts, including Michael Marcagi, Bella Kay, and Mon Rovîa on select legs.
