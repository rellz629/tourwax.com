## What "The Broken Bottle" Actually Is

This is not a normal theater tour with a themed backdrop. Each venue is dressed as The Broken Bottle, the desert honky-tonk bar that runs through Lord Huron's fictional universe, so the room itself becomes the setting rather than just the stage. The band described the run as a neon and moonlit journey across the years.

The practical version: the sets are built around fan favorites, the hits, and deep cuts the band rarely plays live. Because there is no support act, there is more room in the night for the rare material than a standard 75-minute headline slot allows. If you have seen [Lord Huron](/artists/lord-huron) only at a festival or on an amphitheater bill, this is a very different show.

The concept also lines up with where the band has been lately. *The Cosmic Selector Vol. 1*, their fifth album, came out on July 18, 2025 and was built around a jukebox premise from frontman Ben Schneider: what if you could pick your fate the way you pick a song. A tour that turns every venue into a bar is the logical next move for that idea. The album features collaborations with Kristen Stewart and Kazu Makino.

## The Rooms Are the Point

Look at the venue column again. Ryman Auditorium seats 2,362. The Chicago Theatre, Kings Theatre, the Orpheum in Vancouver, the Paramount in Seattle and The Wiltern are all vintage rooms in the 2,000 to 3,500 range. This is a band that headlined [Red Rocks](/venues/red-rocks-amphitheatre) earlier in 2026 playing buildings a fraction of that size.

That is why the two-night stands exist. Chicago, Montreal, Brooklyn, Vancouver and Seattle each get two nights, and Los Angeles now has three, because one night in a theater does not come close to covering demand in those markets. It also means that if your city is a two-night stop, the second night is usually the softer ticket.

A few stops worth flagging:

**Nashville, December 1.** The opener, at the Ryman. Opening nights of a new production tend to be the loosest, and the Ryman is the smallest marquee room on the entire run.

**Sacramento, January 1.** A New Year's Day show at Channel 24, which is one of the newest venues on the schedule. It is also the only date on the tour that lands on a holiday.

**Las Vegas, January 2.** The Theater at Virgin Hotels is the outlier booking here, a hotel theater rather than a historic downtown house.

**Los Angeles, January 10 through 12.** Three nights at The Wiltern to finish, in the band's home city. Expect the loosest setlists of the tour here.

## Tickets

General on-sale ran on August 7, 2026, three weeks before this was written, so what is left is remaining primary inventory plus resale. Two things are specific to this run:

- **No lawn, no upper bowl, no cheap seats by default.** These are seated theaters, most of them under 3,000 capacity. There is no low-tier inventory to fall back on the way there is at an amphitheater show, which is the single biggest difference from the band's summer touring.
- **Second and third nights are the value.** Where a city has multiple dates, the first night almost always clears first. Chicago on December 9, Montreal on December 16, Brooklyn on December 19, Vancouver on January 6, Seattle on January 8, and the two later Wiltern nights are the ones worth checking before you pay resale for a Friday.

## FAQ

### Who is opening for Lord Huron on the Broken Bottle tour?

Nobody. Every date is billed as "An Evening with Lord Huron" with no opening act. This is confirmed on the band's official announcement and on individual venue listings, including the Ryman Auditorium page for the December 1 opener.

### How many shows are on 23 Nights at the Broken Bottle?

Twenty-three were announced on August 4, 2026, which is where the tour name comes from. A third Los Angeles night at The Wiltern was added afterward on January 12, 2027, bringing the current total to 24 shows across 17 cities.

### When does the tour start and end?

It opens Tuesday, December 1, 2026 at the Ryman Auditorium in Nashville and finishes at The Wiltern in Los Angeles, on January 11 as originally announced or January 12 with the added night.

### Which cities get more than one night?

Chicago (December 8 and 9), Montreal (December 15 and 16), Brooklyn (December 18 and 19), Vancouver (January 5 and 6), Seattle (January 7 and 8), and Los Angeles (January 10, 11 and 12).

### Is this a residency at one venue?

No. It is a traveling residency, which is why the band calls it "A Rambling Residency." The production moves city to city and each venue is transformed into the same fictional bar, rather than the band setting up in one building for a long run.

### When did tickets go on sale?

The artist presale started Wednesday, August 5, 2026 at noon local time, and the general public on-sale was Friday, August 7 at 10 a.m. local time. Tickets went out through Ticketmaster, AXS and the individual venues depending on the market.

### Is Lord Huron touring anywhere else in 2027?

Nothing beyond the January dates has been announced. The Broken Bottle run follows the band's summer 2026 North American tour behind *The Cosmic Selector Vol. 1*, and January 12 at The Wiltern is currently the last scheduled show on the books.
