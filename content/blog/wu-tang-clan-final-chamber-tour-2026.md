Wu-Tang Clan has announced a massive 26-city North American run as part of their "Wu-Tang Forever: The Final Chamber" farewell tour. The leg kicks off August 27 in Darien Center, New York and wraps up October 4 in Phoenix, Arizona, with Bone Thugs-N-Harmony joining as special guests.

## The Lineup

The Wu-Tang Clan lineup for this run features original members RZA, GZA, Raekwon, Ghostface Killah, Method Man, Inspectah Deck, U-God, and Masta Killa, performing alongside Cappadonna and Young Dirty Bastard, who stands in for his late father Ol' Dirty Bastard.

Adding Bone Thugs-N-Harmony as support makes this one of the most stacked [Hip-Hop](/tours/hip-hop) bills of the year. Both groups defined the sound of '90s rap and have deep catalogs that translate powerfully to the live stage.

## Why This Tour Matters

This is being billed as a farewell, and Wu-Tang Clan has earned every bit of the send-off. Since forming on Staten Island in 1992, the group has shaped the landscape of hip-hop with genre-defining albums like *Enter the Wu-Tang (36 Chambers)* and *Wu-Tang Forever*. Their influence extends far beyond music into film, fashion, and culture.

Farewell tours in hip-hop are rare. Unlike rock bands that have made the farewell tour a tradition, hip-hop acts don't often frame a tour as the final chapter. That makes this run feel genuinely significant for fans who want to see all the surviving members perform together one last time.
