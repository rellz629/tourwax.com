[Olivia Rodrigo](/artists/olivia-rodrigo) is taking the Unraveled Tour around the world from September 2026 through May 2027, and the support bill is one of the most interesting parts of the whole run. Eighty-six shows, twenty-eight cities, and five different opening acts rotating across the legs. Here's who's opening each stretch of the tour and what to expect when the doors open.

## How the Lineup Works

Unraveled is Rodrigo's third concert tour, following the Sour Tour in 2022 and the GUTS World Tour in 2024 and 2025. Rather than touring with one opener for the entire run, the booking team paired her with five different supporting acts who each take a defined stretch of the calendar. The North American fall and winter legs use two openers in sequence, the residencies in Los Angeles and New York rotate two more, and the European leg splits between the last two.

The result is a tour that feels different depending on which city you catch it in. Each opener leans into a different corner of the alternative and indie-pop world Rodrigo has been pulling from since her debut.

## The Opening Acts

### Wolf Alice

Wolf Alice opens the North American fall leg, from Hartford on September 25 through Columbus on October 30. The London alt-rock quartet won the Mercury Prize in 2018 for *Visions of a Life* and just released their fourth studio album, *The Clearing*, in August 2025 through RCA and Columbia. The record debuted at number one on the UK Albums Chart and earned them a fourth straight Mercury Prize nomination, making Wolf Alice the first act in the prize's history to be nominated for every album they've released.

Their sound has matured into classic pop-rock songwriting with serious hooks, which makes them a strong tonal match for the louder, rock-leaning material on Rodrigo's recent records.

### Devon Again

Devon Again takes over for the late fall and winter North American dates, from Philadelphia on November 7 through Las Vegas on December 20, before continuing as a co-opener with The Last Dinner Party for the Los Angeles residency at Intuit Dome in January 2027.

A twenty-one-year-old LA-based alt-pop artist, Devon Again broke through in 2021 with the TikTok hit "Suburbia" and has built a catalog of R&B-tinged, indie-pop songs since. Her aesthetic sits comfortably alongside Rodrigo's, which is part of why the pairing makes sense for arena crowds full of fans who found both artists through social platforms first.

### The Last Dinner Party

The Last Dinner Party split the Intuit Dome run in January 2027 and then take over solo for the New York residency at Barclays Center in February. The London-based group won the BRITs Rising Star Award in 2024, the BBC Sound of 2024 poll, and Best New Artist at the 2025 BRITs. Their debut, *Prelude to Ecstasy*, hit number one on the UK Albums Chart in February 2024 with the highest first-week sales for a debut album in nearly a decade, and the follow-up *From the Pyre* landed at number two in October 2025.

Their theatrical, baroque-pop staging is a different visual language from the rest of the tour, which makes the New York and LA residencies stand out on the calendar.

### Grace Ives

Grace Ives opens the first half of the European leg: Stockholm on March 19, Amsterdam on March 23, Munich on April 1, and the first stretch of London's O2 Arena residency from April 5 through 9.

A Brooklyn-raised singer and producer, Ives makes compact electro-pop drawn from 2000s R&B and millennial pop, and her second album *Janky Star* picked up a Pitchfork Best New Music tag in 2022. Her sound is sparser and stranger than the other openers on the bill, which gives the European leg a noticeably different opening tone.

### Die Spitz

Die Spitz close out the tour, taking over for the second half of the London residency starting April 12 and continuing through Paris, Milan, Barcelona, and the final London shows ending May 10.

The Austin, Texas four-piece signed to Jack White's Third Man Records in early 2025 and released their debut LP *Something to Consume* in September 2025, produced by Will Yip. Their live shows are loud, frantic, and known for stunts including crowd-surfing solos and rafter climbs, which sets up a high-energy contrast right before Rodrigo's set.

## What to Expect

Unraveled sold out across all eighty-six dates within minutes of going on sale, and the residency-heavy schedule, with multi-night stands in nearly every city, is built to handle the demand without expanding to stadiums. The tradeoff is that ticket scarcity has pushed resale prices well above the original face value in most markets.

The opening lineup is the real signal of how Rodrigo is positioning this era. Wolf Alice and The Last Dinner Party both bring serious critical credentials and rock-leaning catalogs, Die Spitz brings volume and chaos, Grace Ives brings the strangest sonic palette of the bunch, and Devon Again brings the most direct alt-pop overlap with Rodrigo's own audience. Catching the tour twice in different legs would actually deliver two very different shows.

The London O2 residency from April 5 through May 10, 2027 is the longest single-venue run on the schedule and includes Rodrigo's tour-closing dates.
