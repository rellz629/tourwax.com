Four acts open for the [Jonas Brothers](/artists/jonas-brothers) on the Burning Up Tour All Over Again: **Deleasa, Magnus Ferrell, Franklin Jonas, and All Time Low**. Deleasa plays every date on the fall run, Magnus Ferrell covers most of them, Franklin Jonas takes the six-show Midwest and Texas swing in November, and All Time Low appears once, at the Hollywood Bowl on November 18.

The band announced the 45-city tour on August 17, 2026, and the support key was published in the official venue announcements the same day. Tickets go on general sale Friday, August 21 at 10 a.m. local time. Below is the opener for every single date.

## Who These Openers Actually Are

### Deleasa

The only act on all 44 fall dates. Deleasa is Mike Deleasa, a pop singer and DJ who has been in and around the Jonas orbit for well over a decade: he is the brother of Danielle Jonas, which makes him Kevin's brother-in-law, and he has toured with the band before. If you are going to any date from Boston onward, this is the set you will see.

### Magnus Ferrell

The heavy-rotation opener, on 34 of the 44 fall dates. Ferrell is 22, the son of Will Ferrell, and he has spent the past year turning a viral single called "Asleep Talking" into an actual career. His music sits somewhere between jazz piano, pop, and R&B, which is a genuinely odd fit alongside a Jonas Brothers nostalgia run and probably the most interesting thing on this bill. He has been candid in interviews about the nepo baby label rather than dodging it.

### Franklin Jonas

Six dates, all in the middle of the country: Kansas City, Nashville, Oklahoma City, Dallas, Austin, and San Antonio. Franklin is the youngest Jonas sibling, known to a generation of fans as Frankie, and this is the first time he has been billed as a support act on a full leg of one of his brothers' arena tours. Expect the loudest crowd reaction of the night before the headliners even walk out.

### All Time Low

One date only: the [Hollywood Bowl](/venues/hollywood-bowl) on November 18. The Baltimore pop-punk band came up in the same mid-2000s scene the Jonas Brothers did, and putting them on the single most unusual venue of the routing is clearly a deliberate choice. If you were going to pick one show on this tour for the support bill alone, this is it.

## About the Tour Itself

The Burning Up Tour All Over Again is a restaging of the original Burnin' Up Tour, which ran from July 4, 2008 in Toronto through March 22, 2009 in San Juan and supported *A Little Bit Longer*. This version is bigger: 45 cities across North America, with the main run going September 25 through December 21, 2026 after the three August shows in New York.

A few routing details are worth flagging before you buy. Toronto is the only Canadian stop, and it is at RBC Amphitheatre, the waterfront venue formerly known as Budweiser Stage, on September 28. That is an outdoor amphitheatre in late September, so dress accordingly. The Chicago-area show is at Allstate Arena in Rosemont rather than downtown, and the Phoenix-area date is at Desert Diamond Arena in Glendale. The Los Angeles date is the Hollywood Bowl, not an arena, which is why it has the only one-off support booking on the tour.

## FAQ

### Who is opening for the Jonas Brothers in 2026?

Deleasa, Magnus Ferrell, Franklin Jonas, and All Time Low. Deleasa plays all 44 fall dates, Magnus Ferrell plays 34 of them, Franklin Jonas plays six dates from Kansas City through San Antonio, and All Time Low plays only the Hollywood Bowl on November 18.

### Is the same opener at every show?

No. Deleasa is the constant, but the second opener rotates. Magnus Ferrell has most of the East Coast, Midwest, and West Coast dates. Franklin Jonas has the November 3 through November 14 stretch. Four early dates, Manchester, Toronto, Washington, and Raleigh, have Deleasa alone.

### Who opens for the Jonas Brothers at the Hollywood Bowl?

All Time Low, along with Magnus Ferrell and Deleasa. It is the only date on the tour where All Time Low appears.

### When does the Burning Up Tour All Over Again start and end?

The fall run opens September 25, 2026 at TD Garden in Boston and closes December 21, 2026 at [Prudential Center](/venues/prudential-center) in Newark. Three sold-out Madison Square Garden shows on August 20, 21, and 22 came first.

### Is Franklin Jonas performing on the whole tour?

No. Franklin Jonas is billed on six dates only: Kansas City on November 3, Nashville on November 5, Oklahoma City on November 9, Dallas on November 10, Austin on November 13, and San Antonio on November 14.

### When do tickets go on sale?

General on-sale is Friday, August 21, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time through Ticketmaster, following presales that began August 18.
