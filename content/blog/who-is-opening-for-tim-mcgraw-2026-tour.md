_Updated August 24, 2026. The tour is in its final month: remaining stops run from Cincinnati (August 27) through the West Palm Beach finale (September 26), with 49 Winchester and Timothy Wayne continuing as support._

If you've got tickets to [Tim McGraw](/artists/tim-mcgraw)'s Pawn Shop Guitar Tour and want to know who's opening, the answer on most nights is **49 Winchester**, the Appalachian roots-country band from Castlewood, Virginia. On select dates the opener is **Timothy Wayne**, a rising country singer who also happens to be McGraw's nephew, and a few shows feature both.

There's a bigger wrinkle, though: three stadium dates on this tour aren't standard amphitheater shows at all. At Hersheypark Stadium, Fenway Park, and Target Field, McGraw is joined by **[The Chicks](/artists/the-chicks)** and **Lady A** for a stacked triple bill. As McGraw put it when the tour was announced: "I dare you to find more hits in one show!"

The 33-date run kicks off July 9 at Bethel Woods Center for the Arts in upstate New York and wraps September 26 in West Palm Beach.

## Meet 49 Winchester

49 Winchester handles the bulk of the opening duties on this tour. The six-piece band comes out of Castlewood, a small town in Russell County, Virginia, and plays a raw blend of Appalachian country, soul, and roots rock. Their breakout song "Russell County Line" is named for home, and albums like *Fortune Favors the Bold* and *Leavings* turned them from a regional favorite into one of the most respected live acts in the Americana world.

They're a natural fit for a McGraw crowd: big, emotional vocals from frontman Isaac Gibson and a band that built its reputation on the road rather than on playlists. If you're on the fence about showing up early, don't be. This is the kind of opener people leave talking about.

## Meet Timothy Wayne, Tim McGraw's Nephew

The other support act is family. Timothy Wayne is McGraw's nephew, a Louisiana-raised country singer who signed his first major record deal with UMG and Capitol in 2024 while still a history student at LSU. His uncle produces his music alongside Byron Gallimore, the same producer behind decades of McGraw and Faith Hill records, though McGraw has said his rule for his nephew was simple: "I'll produce you, but that's it. You have to forge your own path."

Wayne got his first taste of arena crowds opening for his uncle in 2024, and early songs like "That'd Be You" have started building him a fanbase of his own. Ticket listings show him on select 2026 dates, including Charleston, Cincinnati, Austin, and Dallas, with some nights featuring both Wayne and 49 Winchester on the bill.

## Which Opener Is Playing My Show?

The short version:

- **Most amphitheater dates:** 49 Winchester opens.
- **Select dates:** Timothy Wayne opens, sometimes alongside 49 Winchester (Ticketmaster listings show him in Charleston, Cincinnati, Austin, and Dallas, among others).
- **Hershey, Boston, and Minneapolis:** The Chicks and Lady A joined McGraw for stadium triple bills (all three have now been played).
- **September 5 in Snowmass Village, CO:** a Jazz Aspen Snowmass festival headline set, with the festival's own Saturday bill (including Shaboozey and Avery Anna) rather than the tour openers.

Support acts can shift city to city over a summer-long run, so check your venue's listing close to showtime. The [Tim McGraw](/artists/tim-mcgraw) page on TourWax carries direct ticket links and current details for every date.
