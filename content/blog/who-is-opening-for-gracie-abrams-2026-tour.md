[Gracie Abrams](/artists/gracie-abrams) announced the Look at My Life Tour on May 28, 2026, and tickets hit general sale on June 5. The 64-date arena run supports her third studio album, *Daughter from Hell*, due July 17, 2026, and stretches from Denver on December 2, 2026 to Barcelona on May 28, 2027. Rather than carrying one opener for six months, Abrams booked ten different support acts, each covering a defined stretch of the calendar. Here's who opens each leg and what to expect from them.

## How the Lineup Works

The tour splits into 37 North American shows from December 2026 through March 2027 and 27 European shows in April and May 2027. Eight openers rotate through the North American leg, usually for a city pair or a short run of dates, and the European leg is split between two more. It's the same residency-style booking logic Olivia Rodrigo used for the Unraveled Tour: the show changes depending on which city you catch it in, and the openers map loosely to the audience in each region.

## The North American Openers

### Rachel Chinouriri

Rachel Chinouriri opens the first stretch, December 2 through 14, covering Denver, Oakland, Glendale, and the first Los Angeles date. The British singer-songwriter broke out with her 2024 debut *What a Devastating Turn of Events* and has spent the last two years becoming one of the most talked-about names in UK indie-pop. Her diary-style writing sits naturally next to Abrams' own.

[Rachel Chinouriri tour dates](/artists/rachel-chinouriri)

### Holly Humberstone

Holly Humberstone takes the remaining Los Angeles dates, December 18 through 20. The BRITs Rising Star winner has history with this exact audience: she opened Olivia Rodrigo's Sour Tour in 2022 and has built a catalog of moody, confessional pop across records like *Paint My Bedroom Black*. For LA crowds, this is one of the strongest pairings on the run.

[Holly Humberstone tour dates](/artists/holly-humberstone)

### Del Water Gap

Del Water Gap, the project of S. Holden Jaffe, covers Seattle and Portland from January 26 through February 1. Jaffe and Abrams go way back as friends and collaborators, which makes this booking feel more personal than promotional. Expect heart-on-sleeve indie-pop with a live band that has grown noticeably tighter over several headline tours.

[Del Water Gap tour dates](/artists/del-water-gap)

### Charlotte Lawrence

Charlotte Lawrence supports the Chicago and Nashville shows, February 11 through 15. The LA-based pop artist has been releasing dark, atmospheric singles since her teens and brings a slightly moodier edge to the bill than the openers around her.

[Charlotte Lawrence tour dates](/artists/charlotte-lawrence)

### Grace Ives

Grace Ives takes the longest single stretch, Toronto through Boston, February 18 through March 2, hitting Atlanta and Charlotte along the way. The Brooklyn electronic-pop artist earned wide critical acclaim for *Janky Star* and is having a big touring year: she is also one of the rotating openers on Olivia Rodrigo's Unraveled Tour. If you want the most sonically adventurous opener on this bill, it's her.

[Grace Ives tour dates](/artists/grace-ives)

### Bella Kay

Bella Kay opens Washington, D.C. and Montreal from March 4 through 9. She's the newest name on the lineup, a rising singer-songwriter getting her first major arena exposure on this run, which is exactly the kind of slot that launched several of the other openers on this list.

[Bella Kay tour dates](/artists/bella-kay)

### Jensen McRae

Jensen McRae supports the Philadelphia shows on March 12 and 13. The LA singer-songwriter first went viral in 2021 with "Immune" and has since grown into one of the sharpest lyricists in the singer-songwriter lane. Her 2025 album cycle pushed her from internet favorite to a genuine headline act, so catching her in an opening slot may not be possible for much longer.

[Jensen McRae tour dates](/artists/jensen-mcrae)

### The Japanese House

The Japanese House closes out the North American leg with the Brooklyn residency, March 16 through 20. Amber Bain's project has been a fixture of UK indie-pop for a decade, with *In the End It Always Does* landing on plenty of best-of lists in 2023. The dreamy, layered production is a natural fit for a multi-night run in one room.

## The European Openers

### Samia

Samia covers the first half of the European leg, from Paris through the London dates, with stops including Antwerp, Dublin, Manchester, and Glasgow. Her albums *The Baby* and *Honey* made her a cult favorite in American indie rock, and European audiences have been catching up fast.

### Jake Minch

Jake Minch takes the back half, Amsterdam through the Barcelona closer on May 28, 2027, with Berlin, Stockholm, and Milan in between. The young singer-songwriter built his audience clip by clip online before proving it translates to a live room, and this run is his biggest stage yet.

[Jake Minch tour dates](/artists/jake-minch)

## What to Expect

This is Abrams' first arena-scale headline tour, following The Secret of Us Tour in 2024 and 2025, and the booking reads like a map of where her sound comes from: British confessional pop, Brooklyn indie, and a couple of openers who came up the same way she did, one viral song at a time. Multi-night stands in Los Angeles, Brooklyn, and London anchor the schedule, and several openers (Holly Humberstone and Grace Ives in particular) have direct crossover with the Olivia Rodrigo touring universe, so fans of one tour will find a lot to like at the other.
