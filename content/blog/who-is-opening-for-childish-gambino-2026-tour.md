[Childish Gambino](/artists/childish-gambino) is in the final chapter. Donald Glover has made clear the Childish Gambino project is winding down, and the 2026 tour dates supporting *Bando Stone and the New World* represent some of the last opportunities to see the act live. The support lineup reflects his taste: eclectic, genre-defying artists who complement the album's cinematic scope. Here's who's on the bill.

## The Opening Acts

### Amaarae

The Ghanaian-American singer appears on select North American dates as direct support. Amaarae was featured on *Bando Stone and the New World* and her presence on the tour makes obvious sense. Her afropop-meets-indie-R&B sound is a natural runway into Gambino's own genre-fluid headlining set. She's one of the most compelling live performers in her lane right now, and catching her in a smaller slot before her own headline moment arrives is worth the price alone.

### Fousheé

The New Jersey singer-songwriter joins the bill on the spring dates. Fousheé's voice is one of the more distinctive in R&B, and she brings a rawness to her live show that hits differently in an arena setting. Her run of indie features and solo material has built a fanbase that overlaps heavily with Gambino's.

### Teezo Touchdown

The Texas-born artist known for his genre-blending rock-meets-R&B style appears on summer festival and amphitheater dates. Teezo's high-energy live show functions as a perfect opening shot for a crowd that's there to see something more than a standard pop concert. His albums have drawn comparisons to the earlier, more rock-influenced end of Gambino's catalog, making the pairing feel intentional.

## Why the Support Bill Matters

Part of what has always defined a Childish Gambino show is curation. From the *Camp* era through *Awaken, My Love!* to *Bando Stone*, he has consistently used the opener slot to introduce audiences to artists that felt like discoveries rather than radio filler. The 2026 lineup continues that tradition.

If you're attending one of these shows, arriving for the opener is not optional. Amaarae especially is the kind of artist you'll be watching headline her own tour within two years.

## The Bando Stone Era

*Bando Stone and the New World* arrived in 2024 as a companion piece to Glover's film of the same name, blending hip-hop, orchestral pop, and Afrobeat into one of the more ambitious albums of his career. The tour has been built to match that scale: arenas and amphitheaters, production designed to feel like a film experience rather than a concert. Attendees have described the staging as unlike any tour currently on the road.

Given Glover's stated intent to wind down the Childish Gambino name, these 2026 dates are likely among the final chances to see the project performed live. That context gives every show a finality that's rare in touring.
