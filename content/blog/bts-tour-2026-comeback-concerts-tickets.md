_Updated April 25, 2026 with the latest information on BTS's group comeback and member solo dates._

After a long wait, 2026 is the year ARMY has been counting down to. With every member of BTS now discharged from mandatory South Korean military service as of mid-2025, the group has reunited and is back to recording and performing as a seven-piece for the first time since 2022. Here is everything to know about BTS's 2026 touring plans, what tickets are likely to cost, and how to be ready the moment dates go on sale.

## Is BTS Touring in 2026?

Yes — and this is the big one. After all seven members (RM, Jin, Suga, J-Hope, Jimin, V, and Jungkook) completed their service obligations, HYBE and BIGHIT confirmed the group is back together and working on a new album for release in 2026, with a global tour planned to support it. This is the first full-group BTS tour since the *Permission to Dance on Stage* run wrapped in Las Vegas and Seoul in 2022.

## When Will BTS 2026 Tour Dates Be Announced?

Industry expectation is that BTS will announce a 2026 world tour in conjunction with the new album release. Past BTS tour announcements have followed the same playbook:

- Album teaser → album release → tour announcement within weeks
- Dates announced in batches: Asia first, then North America, then Europe and Latin America
- Pre-sales tied to Weverse membership and the BTS fan club, followed by general on-sale

Watching Weverse, the official BTS social accounts, and HYBE press releases is the most reliable way to catch the announcement window.

## What Will BTS 2026 Tickets Cost?

BTS tickets sit at the top end of K-pop pricing. Based on the 2022 *Permission to Dance on Stage* tour and adjusted for current market pricing:

| Ticket Type | Expected Price Range |
|---|---|
| Upper level / nosebleeds | $150 – $300 |
| Lower bowl | $400 – $800 |
| Floor / near stage | $800 – $1,500 |
| Premium / VIP packages | $1,500 – $4,000+ |
| Resale (premium markets) | $1,000 – $10,000+ |

Stadium-only routing means demand will be intense — most shows will sell out the moment general on-sale opens, and resale prices will climb fast.

## How to Get BTS 2026 Tickets

Given the level of demand, planning ahead is the difference between getting a ticket and watching the show on YouTube clips.

**Sign up for Weverse and the official BTS fan club.** Pre-sales for past tours have been Weverse-gated, with priority access for paid fan club members. The fan club membership window typically opens months before tour announcements, so sign up early.

**Have multiple ticketing accounts ready.** Ticketmaster, AXS, and regional ticketing platforms each cover different venues. Have accounts active and verified before on-sale day.

**Be flexible on city.** If your hometown date sells out, secondary cities sometimes have better availability — and travel costs can be less than premium resale pricing.
