Fall 2026 is shaping up to be one of the busiest touring seasons in years. Below is a roundup of major tours with dates and locations now listed and on sale, spanning R&B, country, metal, pop, and rock. Every artist links to their TourWax page, where you can see the full city-by-city routing with tickets from Ticketmaster and SeatGeek.

For a rolling view of what just hit the market, check [what's on sale today](/concerts/on-sale-today).

## R&B and Hip-Hop

### Kehlani

The biggest new routing of the season. [Kehlani](/artists/kehlani)'s world tour hits North America starting August 6 in Minneapolis and runs through early October, closing at Shoreline Amphitheatre in the Bay Area, with stops including Los Angeles, New York, Boston, Atlanta, and Dallas. Durand Bernarr, Isaia Huron, TheARTI$t, and WASEEL support on select dates.

### NAV

The Toronto rapper has a full North American run on the books for September into early October. Dates and cities are on the [NAV artist page](/artists/nav).

### Keyshia Cole

A late-summer theater and arena run from late August through mid-September. Full routing on the [Keyshia Cole page](/artists/keyshia-cole).

## Country

### Ella Langley

Fresh off her run opening for Morgan Wallen, [Ella Langley](/artists/ella-langley) has headline dates listed from August 2026 all the way into spring 2027, including a marquee night at Red Rocks on October 7 with Kameron Marlowe supporting. She is one of the fastest-rising headliners in country music, and these rooms will not stay available long.

### The Red Clay Strays

The Alabama band's rise continues with dates running from early August through December. See the full schedule on [The Red Clay Strays page](/artists/the-red-clay-strays).

### Jamey Johnson

The outlaw country stalwart has shows listed from mid-August through mid-December. Routing is on the [Jamey Johnson page](/artists/jamey-johnson).

## Rock and Metal

### Black Veil Brides

A long run kicks off in late August and stretches into February 2027. Dates on the [Black Veil Brides page](/artists/black-veil-brides).

### August Burns Red

The metalcore veterans tour from late September into January 2027. Full list on the [August Burns Red page](/artists/august-burns-red).

### Pennywise

The punk lifers have a tight fall run from late September to mid-October. Dates on the [Pennywise page](/artists/pennywise).

### State Champs

The pop-punk favorites close out the year with a November-December run. Routing on the [State Champs page](/artists/state-champs).

### Suede

A rare stateside treat: the Britpop icons have North American dates listed for the fall. Details on the [Suede page](/artists/suede).

## Pop and Blues

### Becky G

The pop and Latin star has dates from early August through October. Full schedule on the [Becky G page](/artists/becky-g).

### Joe Bonamassa

The blues guitar titan's routing runs from August 2026 into March 2027, including a Red Rocks date in late August. Dates on the [Joe Bonamassa page](/artists/joe-bonamassa).
