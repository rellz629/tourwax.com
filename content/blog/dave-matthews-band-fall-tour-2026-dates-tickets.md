## What Makes This Run Different

DMB is an amphitheater band by habit. The summer tour is the institution: lawn seats, Gorge weekends, a rotating setlist across two and three night stands outdoors. A seven-date November arena run is the exception, not the pattern, and it changes the experience in ways that matter if you have only ever seen the band outside.

Three things are worth knowing:

**No lawn.** Every seat is a ticketed seat inside a building. There is no cheap grass option to fall back on, which is part of why the best dates cleared so quickly.

**Tighter rooms.** The Kohl Center in Madison and Amica Mutual Pavilion in Providence are the two smallest venues on the run by a wide margin. Providence in particular is the kind of booking that DMB fans travel for, because the band has spent most of the last decade in far larger rooms in the Northeast.

**Short routing, no filler.** Seven shows in ten days with only one market repeated. There is no second night to absorb demand anywhere except New York, and the New York second night is the one that already sold out.

## The Venues

### Kohl Center, Madison

The tour opener, and a college basketball arena on the University of Wisconsin campus rather than a purpose-built concert room. Opening nights of a DMB run tend to be looser and heavier on material the band has not played in a while, since nothing has settled into a groove yet. See what else is booked at [Kohl Center](/venues/kohl-center).

### Bridgestone Arena, Nashville

Friday night downtown, in a building that hosts more country than jam. Check the full [Bridgestone Arena schedule](/venues/bridgestone-arena).

### United Center, Chicago

Sold out. The Saturday of the run and the largest capacity outside New York. If you are chasing this one, resale is the only route, and the [United Center](/venues/united-center) page tracks live listings.

### Amica Mutual Pavilion, Providence

The smallest room on the tour and the only Monday night. By DMB standards this is a genuinely intimate show.

### CFG Bank Arena, Baltimore

The Tuesday date, in a downtown arena that reopened in 2023 after a gut renovation. Browse [CFG Bank Arena](/venues/cfg-bank-arena).

### Madison Square Garden, New York

Two nights to finish, November 12 and 13, with the 13th already sold out. DMB has a long history at [Madison Square Garden](/venues/madison-square-garden), and a two-night stand there should mean two very different setlists. The band is known for avoiding repeats across consecutive nights in the same building.

## Tickets and Pricing

General on-sale ran August 21. What is left now is a mix of remaining primary inventory on the five shows that did not sell out and resale on all seven.

A few practical notes for this specific run:

- **The two sold-out dates behave differently.** Chicago and the second Garden night will only get more expensive as November approaches, because there is no supply relief coming. If you need one of those, buying early is usually cheaper than waiting.
- **The midweek dates are the value.** Monday in Providence and Tuesday in Baltimore are the least contested nights on the routing.
- **Watch for production holds.** Arena shows routinely release blocked seats behind and beside the stage in the final week. That is the one legitimate way sold-out shows get cheap seats back.
