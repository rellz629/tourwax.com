_Updated July 25, 2026._

If you're searching "Eminem tour 2026," you've probably seen posts claiming a Final World Tour, a Monster Tour revival, or stadium dates about to go on sale. Here is the honest answer, based on what's actually listed on Ticketmaster and SeatGeek right now.

## Is Eminem Touring in 2026?

**No. As of July 2026, Eminem has not announced a tour, and there are no Eminem headline dates on sale through any official ticketing outlet.** TourWax pulls live event data from Ticketmaster and SeatGeek daily, and the [Eminem page on TourWax](/artists/eminem) currently shows no upcoming shows. If that changes, confirmed dates will appear there first.

This is consistent with how Eminem has operated for years. He has not mounted a full headline tour since the *Rapture* dates in 2019, and *The Death of Slim Shady (Coup de Grace)* was released in 2024 without a supporting tour. His live appearances have been concentrated in one-off festival headline slots, hometown Detroit events, and televised performances rather than multi-city runs.

## What About the "Final World Tour" and "Monster Tour 2026" Posts?

Those are not real announcements. Viral posts circulating on Facebook and unofficial fan sites have claimed Eminem is launching a farewell world tour or reviving The Monster Tour in 2026. None of this has come from Eminem's official channels, Shady Records, or any legitimate ticketing partner. Some of these pages exist purely to farm engagement, and some link to ticket sites that don't hold real inventory.

A simple rule keeps you safe: if a date isn't on Ticketmaster, it isn't real. When Eminem announces anything, it will hit his official site and socials, and tickets will sell through major primary outlets.

## Who Would Open for Eminem If He Tours?

A huge share of searches around Eminem touring are about support acts. There's no 2026 lineup to report because there's no tour, but Eminem's history gives a reliable picture of who he brings on the road:

- **Big Sean**: One of the most frequent Detroit collaborators and tour companions
- **Skylar Grey**: Has appeared on multiple Eminem runs as a featured vocalist
- **50 Cent**: Joint billing has happened at select festival and event shows
- **Royce da 5'9"**: Detroit native and longtime creative partner

Any future run would very likely lean on Shady Records artists and Detroit-area acts, as his stadium shows always have.

## Where Might You Actually See Eminem Live?

Even without a tour, Eminem surfaces live in a few predictable places:

1. **Festival headline slots.** His rare live appearances in recent years have mostly been festival one-offs. Major lineup announcements are the first place to look.
2. **Detroit events.** Hometown shows and special events in Michigan have always been his most likely stage.
3. **Televised and one-off performances.** Halftime shows, award shows, and anniversary events occasionally draw him out.

Keep an eye on the [Eminem artist page](/artists/eminem), which updates automatically the moment any ticketed date appears.

## The Death of Slim Shady Era

*The Death of Slim Shady (Coup de Grace)* arrived in 2024 as Eminem's twelfth studio album, framed as the formal retirement of the Slim Shady character. It debuted at number one in multiple countries and would be the obvious catalyst for a tour, but two years on, no supporting run has materialized. At this point, any 2026 or 2027 live activity is more likely to look like high-profile one-offs than a traditional album tour.
