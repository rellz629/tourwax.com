Karol G closed her Coachella Weekend 2 headline set on April 20, 2026 with a major announcement: she's going on tour. It's her first tour in two years, and the crowd at the Empire Polo Club reacted exactly how you'd expect.

The announcement came at the end of what was already a historic set — Karol G became the first Colombian artist to headline Coachella, capping a run that has made her one of the biggest live draws in Latin music. Dates, cities, and venue details are rolling out now.

## Why This Tour Is a Big Deal

Karol G's last full tour cycle was one of the highest-grossing Latin music tours in history. The *Mañana Será Bonito* stadium run in 2024 sold out arenas and stadiums across North America and Latin America, cementing her status alongside Bad Bunny as the defining voice of reggaeton's global moment.

Two years off the road means pent-up demand is enormous. Her Coachella performance drew massive viewership on the livestream and trended globally within minutes of the tour announcement. Expect tickets to move fast when they go on sale.

## What to Expect at a Karol G Show

Karol G's live shows are full production events. Her previous tours featured elaborate stage design, costume changes, elaborate choreography, and a setlist that mixes her reggaeton and urban pop hits with slower moments that showcase her vocal range.

Hits to expect in the setlist include:

- *Tusa* (with Nicki Minaj on record, often covered live)
- *Provenza*
- *Mamiii*
- *Cairo*
- *La Bichota*
- *El Makinon*
- *Tonight*
- Material from *Mañana Será Bonito* and *MAÑANA SERÁ BONITO (BICHOTA SEASON)*

Her shows are known for high energy from the first song through the encore. Plan for 90 minutes to two hours of performance time at full production venues.
