The Chicks are back on the road in 2026 with a mix of headline shows, stadium dates with Tim McGraw, and festival appearances. If you're wondering who's opening for The Chicks this year, or who they're sharing a stage with, here's the full breakdown.

## The Chicks' 2026 Tour at a Glance

The Chicks have three types of shows on their 2026 calendar:

1. **Headline casino shows** in June (Highland and Lincoln, CA)
2. **Stadium shows** as special guests on Tim McGraw's Pawn Shop Guitar Tour (July–August)
3. **Festival appearances** at Zootown Music Festival and Boots and Hearts (June–August)

Each type has a different lineup, so who you'll see depends on which show you attend.

## Tim McGraw's Pawn Shop Guitar Tour: Stadium Shows

The biggest shows on The Chicks' 2026 schedule are three stadium dates as special guests on Tim McGraw's Pawn Shop Guitar Tour. These are the shows where the full lineup is confirmed:

- **Friday, July 11** - Hersheypark Stadium, Hershey, PA
- **Thursday, July 30** - Fenway Park, [Boston](/concerts/boston), MA
- **Saturday, August 23** - Target Field, Minneapolis, MN

### The Lineup

- **Tim McGraw:** headliner
- **The Chicks:** special guest
- **Lady A:** special guest
- **49 Winchester:** support (select dates)
- **Timothy Wayne:** support (select dates)

This is one of the biggest country touring packages of 2026. Tim McGraw headlines, with [The Chicks](/artists/the-chicks) and Lady A both playing full sets at these three stadium shows. The pairing brings together three of country music's biggest names across different eras: McGraw's hitmaking catalog, The Chicks' return to touring, and Lady A's crossover appeal.

49 Winchester, the Virginia-based country-rock band, and McGraw's nephew Timothy Wayne rotate as openers across the broader 33-date Pawn Shop Guitar Tour, and may also appear on the stadium bills.

## What to Expect at a Chicks Show in 2026

The Chicks (Natalie Maines, Martie Maguire, and Emily Strayer) are touring behind decades of hits including "Wide Open Spaces," "Cowboy Take Me Away," "Not Ready to Make Nice," and material from their 2020 album *Gaslighter*. Their live shows are known for tight harmonies, instrumental virtuosity (Maguire on fiddle, Strayer on banjo and guitar), and Maines' powerhouse vocals.

Whether you catch them headlining a casino, sharing a stadium with Tim McGraw and Lady A, or closing out a festival, expect a setlist that spans their full career.
