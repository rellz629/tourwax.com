_Updated August 24, 2026 with the confirmed fall openers: Macklemore takes over lead support from September 4, with Lukas Graham and Aaron Rowe rounding out the bill._

[Ed Sheeran](/artists/ed-sheeran) brings the Loop Tour to North American stadiums through the back half of 2026, touring his 2025 album Play across a run that stretches from June into November. Like a lot of stadium tours at this scale, the support slot is not one act for the whole thing. Sheeran rotates a deep bench of openers, so the undercard changes depending on which city and night you catch. Here is who is opening, what each act brings, and where the tour heads next.

## How the Support Lineup Works

Sheeran does not carry a single opener across the entire North American run. Instead, a group of artists splits the calendar, with each act covering a stretch of dates rather than the whole tour. Some nights stack two or three support acts, so the bill in one city can look very different from the show two states over. The smart move is to check the specific date before you buy, since the opener is the part of the night most likely to change from stop to stop.

Through the summer, the constant was Myles Smith, the rising British singer-songwriter who anchored the marquee dates through Detroit on August 29. For the fall leg, the bill settles into a steady trio: Macklemore as lead support, Lukas Graham in the middle slot, and Aaron Rowe opening the night (with BIIRD in that slot through the East Rutherford stand).

## The 2026 Openers

### Myles Smith

The most frequent name on the summer bill, Smith broke through on the strength of folk-pop singles built for exactly this kind of room. He anchored the biggest stadium nights, including Nashville and Chicago, and his run ends with the Detroit show at Ford Field on August 29. He is not on the fall dates.

### Macklemore

The Seattle rapper brings the highest-wattage name on the undercard, and from September 4 onward he is the lead support for the rest of the US run: East Rutherford, Philadelphia, Foxborough, Atlanta, Indianapolis, Charlotte, Arlington, and the Tampa closer. His catalog of arena-sized hits makes him closer to a co-headliner on the nights he plays.

### Lukas Graham

The Danish band behind a run of global pop hits are the other fall constant, playing every remaining stadium date from Detroit through Tampa. Their big, hook-driven songs are a natural fit for a stadium warmup.

### Sigrid

The Norwegian pop star takes the support slot in Las Vegas, bringing the kind of high-energy, synth-forward set that translates well to a stadium crowd settling in for the night.

### Amble

[Amble](/artists/amble) is the Irish folk trio adding an acoustic, harmony-rich dimension to the early part of the evening on dates including Nashville and Chicago. They are worth showing up early for if you love Sheeran's quieter, songwriter side.

### Aaron Rowe

[Aaron Rowe](/artists/aaron-rowe) is the Dublin singer-songwriter who appears on more North American dates than almost anyone else on the roster. From Philadelphia on September 19 through the end of the tour he holds the opening slot every night, and he is the sole support for the special two-night Hard Rock Live run in Hollywood, Florida. He is one of the newer names getting a major platform on this tour.

### Mark Ambor

Ambor brings a sunny, feel-good pop sound to select dates and is a fitting opener for the singalong atmosphere these shows are built around.

### Ellie Banke

One of the up-and-coming names on the bill, Banke opened a handful of summer dates including Chicago and Minneapolis, an early-evening warmup for fans filing in.

### BIIRD

The Irish group hold the opening slot on the late-summer dates, including Detroit and both MetLife Stadium nights, before Aaron Rowe takes over the slot from Philadelphia onward.
