Two of R&B's biggest names are sharing a stage this summer. Usher and Chris Brown have announced The R&B Tour, a 33-date co-headlining stadium run across North America produced by Live Nation. The tour kicks off June 26 in Denver and wraps up December 11 in Tampa, making stops in virtually every major market along the way.

## Tickets and On-Sale Dates

Tickets are not yet on general sale, but here's the full timeline:

- **Citi Pre-sale:** April 21 at 12 p.m. local time
- **R&B Tour Pre-sale:** April 23
- **General Public On Sale:** April 27 at 12 p.m. local time

VIP packages are available and include premium tickets, a behind-the-scenes tour, access to a pre-show VIP Lounge, and exclusive VIP merchandise.

## Why This Tour Is a Must-See

Joint tours between two headliner-level artists are rare, and this one pairs two of the most compelling live performers in R&B. Usher's Super Bowl LVIII halftime show in 2024 reminded the world what he's capable of as a performer: tight choreography, a catalog that spans decades, and the kind of stage presence that fills a stadium. His subsequent "Past Present Future" tour sold out arenas across the country.

Chris Brown has been one of the top-grossing touring acts in R&B for over a decade. His shows are known for their production scale, extended setlists drawing from nearly 20 years of hits, and a live energy that makes arenas feel like clubs. He matches Usher as both a vocalist and a dancer, which makes a shared stage a genuine competition in the best sense.

Between them, you're looking at a setlist that could include "Yeah!", "Confessions Part II", "U Got It Bad", "Burn", "OMG", "Forever", "With You", "No Guidance", "Loyal", "Under the Influence" and dozens more.

## Don't Wait on Tickets

Stadium shows at this level move fast, especially in markets like Los Angeles, New York, Atlanta, and Chicago. Multi-night stands in cities like Detroit, Washington DC, East Rutherford, Toronto, Las Vegas, and Atlanta signal strong demand already built in. If this tour is coming to your city, it's worth setting a reminder for the April 21 pre-sale rather than waiting for general on-sale.
