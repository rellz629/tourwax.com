[Radiohead](/artists/radiohead) will play **21 shows in 2027**, all of them in Australia and Japan: four nights in Perth, six in Melbourne, six in Sydney, and five in Saitama, running from **May 3 through June 13, 2027**. There are **no North American dates**, and there will not be any. The band posted the itinerary on September 7, 2026 with an unusually blunt note attached: "There will be no extra dates announced. These are the only shows that Radiohead will be playing in 2027."

That answers the question most American fans have been asking since the band returned to the stage in late 2025. Radiohead has not played North America since **August 1, 2018**, at Philadelphia's Wells Fargo Center, now Xfinity Mobile Arena, and the 2027 calendar does not change that. The band did add one softening line: "The band hope to do more shows elsewhere in the future."

## Why There Are No North American Dates

This is not a routing accident. In a March 2026 Rolling Stone interview, guitarist Ed O'Brien described the band's new touring model plainly: "What we're going to do is, every year we're going to do a different continent, and we're going to do 20 shows each year. No more, no less." His reasoning was about preservation rather than scarcity: "We want to give absolutely everything each night. We do not ever want it to be like we're going through the motions or we're having to run on empty."

The 2027 announcement follows that template almost exactly. The 2025 return was Europe: 20 shows across Madrid, Bologna, London, Copenhagen and Berlin between November and December. The 2027 run is Asia and Oceania, and at 21 shows it is one night over the stated quota. O'Brien himself has since told fans to take the number "with a pinch of salt," so the arithmetic is a guideline, not a contract.

What that model does mean for American and Canadian fans is straightforward: North America is a future continent on the rotation, not a 2027 one. O'Brien has said the routing plan takes the band to North America, South America and Asia and Oceania, but no dates, cities or year have been announced for a North American run. Anyone selling you a Radiohead 2027 US ticket is selling you something that does not exist.

If and when a North American leg is announced, dates will surface on the [Radiohead artist page](/artists/radiohead) here alongside the rest of our [rock tour listings](/tours/rock).

## How Tickets Work

There is no conventional on-sale for these shows. Both markets run on a registration system, and registering is the only way in.

| Market | Registration Window | What Happens Next |
|--------|--------------------|-------------------|
| Australia | Opened Sep 7 (3:00 p.m. AWST / 5:00 p.m. AEST), closes Sep 10 | Unlock codes sent Sep 11; tickets on sale Sep 15 at 7:00 a.m. local |
| Japan | Opened Sep 7 (4:00 p.m. JST), closes Sep 27 at 11:59 p.m. JST | Successful applicants notified Oct 3 at 1:00 p.m. JST |

Both markets cap you at **four tickets**: per registration in Australia, per application per performance in Japan. Australian sales run through Frontier Touring, and Japanese applications go through eplus. Registration for both starts at [radiohead.com](https://radiohead.com/), with Australian details also on the [Frontier Touring page](https://www.frontiertouring.com/radiohead).

Reported ticket prices land between AUD $139 and $349 for seated tickets and AUD $199 for standing in Australia, with Japanese tickets at ¥15,000 to ¥30,000 seated and ¥26,000 standing. Those figures come from the announcement coverage rather than a posted price grid, so treat them as a range and confirm at checkout.

The Australian window is the urgent one. If you are reading this on September 8, registration closes in roughly two days, and missing it means you cannot buy in the September 15 sale at all.

## What the 2025 Shows Suggested

The European run was the band's first touring since 2018, and it set expectations worth carrying into 2027. Radiohead played multi-night stands in a single city rather than one-offs, which let the setlist move meaningfully from night to night instead of locking into a fixed greatest-hits shape. Fans who caught two nights in the same city generally got two substantially different shows.

The residency format also creates a practical calculation for anyone traveling. Six nights in Sydney and six in Melbourne means far more inventory in one place than a single arena date would offer, and it makes a two-night trip a realistic plan rather than a lottery win. That cuts against the assumption that a 21-show world calendar automatically means impossible tickets.

One caution from 2025: the Copenhagen dates were postponed by two weeks after Thom Yorke developed a throat infection. A compressed residency schedule leaves little slack, so build a little flexibility into non-refundable travel.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Radiohead touring North America in 2027?

No. The band's own announcement states that the Australia and Japan shows are the only concerts Radiohead will play in 2027, and no US or Canadian dates exist. The last Radiohead concert in North America was August 1, 2018 in Philadelphia.

### When will Radiohead play in the US again?

No date has been announced. Ed O'Brien has described a plan to tour a different continent each year, and has named North America as part of the eventual routing, but the band has not committed to a year, a city, or a number of shows. Any specific US date you see quoted for 2027 is not official.

### How many shows are on the 2027 Radiohead tour?

Twenty-one: four in Perth, six in Melbourne, six in Sydney and five in Saitama, Japan.

### Who is opening for Radiohead in 2027?

No support acts have been announced for any of the 21 dates. If openers are added before May, they will typically be confirmed closer to the shows rather than at announcement.

### Can I still register for tickets?

The Australian registration window closes September 10, 2026, with the ticket sale following on September 15. The Japanese application window runs longer, through September 27, 2026, with results announced October 3. Both are handled at radiohead.com.

### Where is Afterpay Arena?

It is the arena at Sydney Olympic Park, called Qudos Bank Arena until July 2026 and still widely known as the Sydney SuperDome. Same building, new name.
