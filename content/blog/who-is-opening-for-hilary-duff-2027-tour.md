**Hilary Duff's 2027 US dates split cleanly in two: [Dashboard Confessional](/artists/dashboard-confessional) opens all ten October shows, and Tegan and Sara open all nine November shows.** In Europe, [Rebecca Black](/artists/rebecca-black) opens every date in May. There is no rotating bill and no city-by-city guessing: if you are holding an October ticket you are seeing Dashboard Confessional, and if you are holding a November ticket you are seeing Tegan and Sara.

Duff announced the 32 new Lucky Me Tour dates on **Wednesday, September 23, 2026**, and the support acts were confirmed in the announcement itself rather than trickling out later. General on-sale for the US leg is **Friday, October 2, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time**. If you want the artist presale, the registration deadline is **Sunday, September 27 at 3 p.m. ET**, which is two days from this post going up.

## The Openers, Leg by Leg

The Lucky Me Tour has now used six different support acts across its run, which is unusual for a single touring cycle. Here is the whole picture, including the legs already on the calendar:

| Europe | May 2027 | Rebecca Black |
| Latin America | Aug 2027 | Not announced |
| United States | Oct 2027 | Dashboard Confessional |
| United States | Nov 2027 | Tegan and Sara |

**Dashboard Confessional** is the booking that says the most about who this tour is actually for. Chris Carrabba's band is the emo-acoustic band of the early 2000s, the same window in which Duff's *Metamorphosis* and *Hilary Duff* records came out, and the October run is built through the northeast and the Rust Belt where that audience is thickest. Expect a singalong set rather than a polite warmup.

**Tegan and Sara** take the November run. The Quin sisters have spent the last decade in the same synth-forward pop register Duff moved into on *Luck... or Something*, and they are a more natural stylistic match for the current album than they would have been for the 2003 version of this bill. November is the western and midwestern half of the tour.

**Rebecca Black** opens all nine European dates in May. Black's post-2021 run as a hyperpop and club artist is one of the more complete reinventions in pop right now, and putting her in front of European arena crowds is the most forward-looking slot on the whole tour.

Neither US opener is billed as a co-headliner. These are standard two-act arena bills with Duff playing the long set.

## Why This Tour Exists

Duff released *Luck... or Something* in 2026, her first studio album in more than a decade, and it debuted at number three on the Billboard 200. The Lucky Me Tour is her first full-scale global headlining tour in close to twenty years, which is why the routing keeps growing rather than settling. What started as a Las Vegas residency at Voltaire in February 2026 and a North American amphitheater run is now a genuine world tour spanning five continents into late 2027.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is opening for Hilary Duff in 2027?

It depends on the leg. Dashboard Confessional opens the ten US dates in October 2027, Tegan and Sara open the nine US dates in November 2027, Rebecca Black opens all nine European dates in May 2027, and Lauren Spencer Smith opens the Canadian dates in January and February 2027.

### Does Dashboard Confessional play every US date?

No. Dashboard Confessional plays the October run only, from Sunrise on October 1 through Pittsburgh on October 16. Tegan and Sara take over for the November run, San Antonio on November 5 through Anaheim on November 20.

### Who opened for Hilary Duff in 2026?

La Roux opened the 2026 North American, UK, Irish, Australian, and New Zealand dates, with Jade LeMac on the 2026 North American shows.

### When do Hilary Duff 2027 tickets go on sale?

General on-sale is Friday, October 2, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time. Citi and Verizon presales start Monday, September 28, the artist presale is Tuesday, September 29, and the Live Nation presale is Thursday, October 1. Artist presale registration closes Sunday, September 27 at 3 p.m. ET.

### Is Hilary Duff playing New York, Los Angeles, or Chicago in 2027?

Not on the announced 2027 US leg. She played all three on the 2026 run, including Madison Square Garden in August 2026. The 2027 itinerary covers Sunrise, Orlando, Raleigh, Baltimore, Uncasville, Hershey, Buffalo, Cleveland, Columbus, Pittsburgh, San Antonio, Fort Worth, Tulsa, Omaha, Milwaukee, Des Moines, Kansas City, Denver, and Anaheim.

### Is there an opener for the Latin American dates?

Not yet. The four August 2027 shows in San Juan, Sao Paulo, Buenos Aires, and Santiago were announced without a support act, as were the February 2027 Mexico dates. We will update this post if that changes.
