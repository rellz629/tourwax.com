Festival of the Lakes is Hammond, Indiana's biggest event of the year: a five-day city festival at Wolf Lake Memorial Park that pairs free admission with arena-level headliners. In recent years the lineup has drawn names you would normally expect at a major amphitheater, all about 25 minutes from downtown Chicago.

Here is a recap of the 2026 edition, what we know about 2027, and how to plan a visit.

## What Is Festival of the Lakes?

Festival of the Lakes is an annual festival run by the City of Hammond, held each July at Wolf Lake Memorial Park (2324 Calumet Avenue). Alongside nightly concerts at the lakefront pavilion, the festival includes carnival rides, food vendors, a fishing derby, fireworks, and family programming.

The part that surprises first-timers: admission is free. You pay for parking (30 dollars per car in 2026), rides, and food, but walking in to see a national headliner costs nothing, which makes it one of the best concert values in the Midwest.

## Planning Your Visit

- **Getting there:** Wolf Lake Memorial Park sits just off I-90 in Hammond, roughly 25 minutes from the Chicago Loop. The pavilion is on the lakefront at Calumet Avenue.
- **Parking:** 30 dollars per car in 2026, cash-heavy lines, and lots fill early on headliner nights. Arrive well before doors for the biggest shows.
- **Seating:** The pavilion area has reserved and VIP options for headline sets, while general lawn viewing is free. Bring a lawn chair or blanket for the grass.
- **Beyond the music:** The carnival midway, food rows, and fireworks make it easy to fill a whole evening even before the headliner starts.
