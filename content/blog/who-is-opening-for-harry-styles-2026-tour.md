Harry Styles' 2026 Together Together Tour is one of the year's most ambitious concert events: 67 shows across just seven cities, including a 30-night residency at Madison Square Garden. Each city gets a different opening act, and the choices say a lot about where Styles is heading musically.

## The Opening Acts by City

Unlike most tours where one opener plays every night, Styles has assigned a unique support act to each city on the tour:

| City | Opener | Dates |
|------|--------|-------|
| Amsterdam | **Robyn** | May 16+ |
| London | **Shania Twain** | Summer 2026 |
| New York City (MSG) | **Jamie xx** | 30 nights |
| Sao Paulo | **Fcukers** | TBA |
| Mexico City | **Jorja Smith** | TBA |
| Melbourne | **Fousheé** | TBA |
| Sydney | **Skye Newman** | TBA |

**Baby J** serves as co-supporting act for the Australian dates.

## Jamie xx at Madison Square Garden (30 Nights)

The headline booking for American fans: Jamie xx opens all 30 nights of Styles' MSG residency. The xx producer and DJ, known for *In Colour* and *In Waves*, brings an electronic set that pairs naturally with Styles' increasingly dance-influenced sound.

A 30-night residency at MSG is rare territory. Billy Joel's ongoing residency is the obvious comparison, but Styles is doing it as a concentrated run rather than spread across years. Jamie xx as the consistent opener gives the residency a cohesive identity. Expect the DJ sets to evolve and shift across the month-long run.

## Robyn in Amsterdam

Robyn opening in Amsterdam is a pairing that makes immediate sense. The Swedish pop icon's catalog, from "Dancing On My Own" to "Call Your Girlfriend," shares DNA with Styles' own blend of pop melancholy and dancefloor energy. Amsterdam's festival-minded audience should eat this up.

## Shania Twain in London

Shania Twain supporting Styles in London is one of the more unexpected pairings on any 2026 tour. But Twain's influence on pop-country crossover is well documented, and Styles has never hidden his appreciation for country music. This one could be special.

## The Rest of the World

- **Jorja Smith** in Mexico City brings UK R&B and jazz influence
- **Fcukers** in Sao Paulo adds punk energy to the bill
- **Fousheé** in Melbourne bridges indie and R&B
- **Skye Newman** in Sydney rounds out the Australian dates

## Tour Structure

The Together Together Tour visits only seven cities worldwide:

1. **Amsterdam**: Johan Cruyff Arena (opens May 16)
2. **London**: Wembley Stadium area
3. **New York City**: Madison Square Garden (30 nights)
4. **Sao Paulo**: TBA
5. **Mexico City**: TBA
6. **Melbourne**: TBA
7. **Sydney**: Accor Stadium (closes December 13)

This residency-based format means if your city isn't on the list, you'll need to travel. But the tradeoff is that each stop becomes an event, and the opener pairings give each city its own character.

## The Album

The tour supports Styles' fourth studio album *Kiss All the Time. Disco, Occasionally.*, and the lead single "Aperture" dropped in January. Expect the setlist to lean heavily on new material alongside hits from *Harry's House* and *Fine Line*.
