[Harry Styles](/artists/harry-styles) has a different opening act in every city on the 2027 leg of the Together, Together Tour. In North America that means **Tinashe** in Glendale, **CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso** in Arlington, **Kylie Minogue** at the Rose Bowl, **[Lainey Wilson](/artists/lainey-wilson)** in Toronto, **Q Marsden** in Atlanta, and **The Womack Sisters** in Chicago. Europe gets Getdown Services, Caroline Polachek, Feist, **[LCD Soundsystem](/artists/lcd-soundsystem)**, Baby Rose, and Tears for Fears.

The 25 new shows were announced on **September 10, 2026**, and the openers are confirmed on Live Nation's official tour page, not rumored. Presale registration for North America closes **Sunday, September 13 at 11:59 p.m. ET**, and general on-sale for the North American dates is **Wednesday, September 16 at 9 a.m. local time**.

## Kylie Minogue at the Rose Bowl Is the Booking People Are Talking About

Kylie Minogue opening two nights at the Rose Bowl is the headline pairing of the North American leg. Minogue has not been a regular support act at any point in her career, and a 90,000 capacity stadium in the [Los Angeles](/concerts/los-angeles) suburbs is an unusual place to see her in a 45 minute slot. If you have wanted to see her live in the United States and have been waiting out a proper headline run, this is the shortest path to it.

The other North American bookings follow the same logic as the 2026 leg: one act per city, chosen to fit the room rather than to fill a slot. Tinashe opening the tour in Glendale gives the first two nights an R&B and club edge. CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso in Arlington is the Argentinian duo whose Tiny Desk set turned into one of the most shared music videos of the last two years. Lainey Wilson in Toronto continues the country crossover thread that ran through Shania Twain's London dates in 2026.

Q Marsden in Atlanta and The Womack Sisters in Chicago are the two least familiar names on the North American bill. The Womack Sisters are the granddaughters of Sam Cooke and the daughters of Cecil Womack and Linda Womack, and they trade in soul harmony rather than pop. Expect a warm, unhurried opening set rather than a hype slot.

## How the 2027 Leg Differs From 2026

The [2026 leg](/blog/who-is-opening-for-harry-styles-2026-tour) was built around residencies: seven cities, 67 shows, and a 30 night stand at Madison Square Garden with Jamie xx opening every night. The 2027 leg inverts that. It is 25 shows across twelve cities, two nights in most of them, in stadiums rather than arenas.

That matters for anyone who missed out in 2026. There are far fewer tickets per city in 2027, but there are nearly twice as many cities. If you did not get into the MSG run, Chicago and Toronto are the closest 2027 substitutes for the Northeast and Midwest, and both are two nights only.

There are no 2027 dates in Australia, New Zealand, Asia, or South America. The Australian shows on this tour are part of the 2026 leg, which is still running.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is opening for Harry Styles at the Rose Bowl in 2027?
Kylie Minogue opens both Rose Bowl Stadium shows in Pasadena on April 23 and April 24, 2027. She is the only opener on the North American leg playing a venue this size in a support slot.

### Who is opening for Harry Styles in Chicago?
The Womack Sisters open both Soldier Field dates, May 28 and May 29, 2027. The soul trio are the granddaughters of Sam Cooke.

### Who is opening for Harry Styles in Toronto?
Lainey Wilson opens both Rogers Stadium shows on May 7 and May 8, 2027, making her the only country act on the 2027 North American leg.

### Does the same opener play every night of the 2027 tour?
No. Each city has its own support act, and that act plays every night in that city. There are twelve different openers across the twelve cities.

### When do Harry Styles 2027 tickets go on sale?
General on-sale is Wednesday, September 16 at 9 a.m. local time for North America and Berlin, Monday, September 14 at 4 p.m. local for Paris, Madrid, Rome, and Dublin, and Thursday, September 17 at 11 a.m. for London. Artist presale registration for North America closes Sunday, September 13 at 11:59 p.m. ET.

### Is Harry Styles playing any US cities besides Glendale, Arlington, Pasadena, Atlanta, and Chicago in 2027?
No. Those are the only announced United States dates, plus Toronto in Canada. Twelve North American shows total, two per city.

### Will more 2027 dates be added?
Nothing further has been announced. The 2026 leg is still running, and the 2027 announcement was presented as the full itinerary for next year. If dates are added, they will appear on the official tour page first.
