[Oasis](/artists/oasis) will play **38 shows in 2027**, running from **May 21 at Celtic Park in Glasgow through September 19 at Knebworth**. The band confirmed the run, branded Oasis Live '27, on **Monday, September 14, 2026**. In the United States there are exactly six dates: three at Gillette Stadium outside [Boston](/concerts/boston) and three at Allegiant Stadium in [Las Vegas](/concerts/las-vegas). **[Richard Ashcroft](/artists/richard-ashcroft) is the special guest at all 38 shows.**

The urgent part is the ticketing. There is no general on-sale for any of these concerts. The only route to a ticket is to register at [oasisinet.com](https://oasisinet.com/), and **registration closes Thursday, September 17, 2026 at 11:00 a.m. ET / 8:00 a.m. PT / 4:00 p.m. BST**. If you are reading this on September 15, that is two days away, and missing it means you cannot buy in the September 25 sale at all.

## How Registration and the On-Sale Work

This is not a normal Ticketmaster drop, and treating it like one is the most common way people are going to miss out.

| Step | When | Detail |
|------|------|--------|
| Register | Now through Thu, Sep 17, 4:00 p.m. BST / 11:00 a.m. ET | Free, at oasisinet.com. Pick up to three cities in order of preference. |
| Codes emailed | Thu, Sep 24 through Sun, Sep 27 | Successful registrants get a unique, non-transferable code naming their assigned sale window. |
| Tickets on sale | Fri, Sep 25 through Sun, Sep 27 | Staggered windows across the three days. In the UK there are three windows per day. |

A few rules worth knowing before you register:

- **Mailing list members still have to register.** Being on the list does nothing on its own.
- **Registration is not a ticket.** It is entry into an allocation. Plenty of registrants will not receive a code.
- **Four tickets per unique code**, and that cap includes anything you later buy on resale.
- **No general on-sale and no other public presales.** The band has said limited tickets may be released later, but nothing is scheduled.
- **Transfer is restricted.** Tickets are mobile wallet entry only, and the only sanctioned resale is Ticketmaster at the original purchase price, plus Twickets in the UK. A ticket rendered outside a mobile wallet will not scan.

That last point matters given what happened around the 2025 run. If a third-party site is selling you an Oasis 2027 ticket today, before codes have even gone out, it does not have one.

## The Cities That Did Not Make the Cut

The 2027 North American plan is much narrower than 2025, and that is the detail most American fans are getting wrong.

The Live '25 reunion tour played Toronto, Chicago, East Rutherford, Los Angeles and Mexico City. **None of those cities are on the 2027 itinerary.** There is no New York date, no Chicago date, no Los Angeles date, and no Canadian or Mexican date at all. Boston and Las Vegas are both new markets for this reunion, and Boston in particular was the subject of persistent 2025 rumors that never produced a show.

Six US concerts for the entire country is a deliberately small number, and it is worth factoring into how you rank your three city preferences at registration. A Las Vegas Tuesday night in August is a very different proposition from a Boston Saturday.

## Knebworth, Again

The tour closes with four nights at Knebworth Park on September 11, 12, 18 and 19, 2027. Oasis played Knebworth twice in August 1996, to roughly 125,000 people each night, with well over two million people reportedly applying for tickets. Those two shows became the high-water mark of the band's career and, for a lot of people, of British guitar music generally.

Doing four nights there in 2027 doubles the 1996 footprint. The two Slane Castle dates the week before give the run a second outdoor-estate stop in Ireland before the finish.

The band's announcement statement, in typically restrained fashion: "START THE CELEBRATIONS!!!! After a period of reflection 'The Most Damaging Pop Cultural Force In Recent British History' has declared itself fit for purpose and will once again return to lift the spirits of the people. Come witness the fitness. It shall be a sight to behold."

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Oasis playing New York in 2027?

No. The only US dates on Oasis Live '27 are three shows at Gillette Stadium near Boston in August and three at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas. There is no New York, New Jersey, Chicago or Los Angeles date, and no Canadian date.

### When is the Oasis 2027 registration deadline?

Thursday, September 17, 2026 at 4:00 p.m. BST / 5:00 p.m. CEST / 8:00 a.m. PT / 11:00 a.m. ET, at oasisinet.com. Registration is the only way to get access to tickets.

### When do Oasis 2027 tickets go on sale?

Across Friday, September 25 through Sunday, September 27, 2026, in staggered windows. Your code email, sent between September 24 and September 27, tells you which window you have. There is no general on-sale.

### Who is opening for Oasis in 2027?

Richard Ashcroft is the special guest at all 38 shows. Ashcroft also supported the band across the UK, Ireland and South America on the 2025 tour. No additional support acts have been announced.

### How many tickets can I buy?

Four per unique code, and that limit includes any tickets you subsequently buy through resale.

### Does registering guarantee me a ticket?

No. Registration puts you in the pool for an allocated sale window. Codes are issued to a subset of registrants, and a code gets you into a queue rather than to a confirmed seat.

### How many Oasis shows are on the 2027 tour?

Thirty-eight: five in Glasgow, eleven in Manchester, two each in Munich, Barcelona, Amsterdam, Paris and Rome, three each in Boston and Las Vegas, two at Slane Castle and four at Knebworth.
