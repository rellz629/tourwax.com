YOASOBI has announced their first North American headlining tour, dubbed the "Never Ending Stories" Tour. The Japanese pop duo, made up of producer Ayase and vocalist ikura, will play 8 dates across the U.S. and Canada, kicking off with a Lollapalooza set Thursday, July 30 in Chicago, IL and wrapping up Monday, August 17 at the Hollywood Bowl in Los Angeles, CA.

## Tour Dates

### July 2026
- **Thursday, July 30** — Grant Park, Chicago, IL

### August 2026
- **Sunday, August 2** — Grant Park, Chicago, IL
- **Wednesday, August 5** — TD Garden, Boston, MA
- **Friday, August 7** — Barclays Center, Brooklyn, NY
- **Sunday, August 9** — TD Coliseum, Hamilton, ON
- **Thursday, August 13** — Climate Pledge Arena, Seattle, WA
- **Saturday, August 15** — Oakland Arena, Oakland, CA
- **Monday, August 17** — Hollywood Bowl, Los Angeles, CA

## What to Expect

The Never Ending Stories Tour marks a significant moment for YOASOBI. After breaking out internationally with anime tie-ins like "Yoru ni Kakeru" and "Idol" (the chart-topping theme from Oshi no Ko), the duo is finally headlining major North American venues in their own right. Expect a heavy mix of fan-favorite anime themes, J-pop deep cuts, and tracks performed in both Japanese and English versions.

Production-wise, YOASOBI's recent shows in Asia have leaned into anime-style projection mapping and synced LED visuals, with ikura fronting a full live band. The Hollywood Bowl closer is shaping up to be the marquee date of the run.

The Lollapalooza appearance on July 30 is technically separate from the headlining run, but it makes for a strong festival kickoff before the duo settles into their own arena dates starting in early August.
