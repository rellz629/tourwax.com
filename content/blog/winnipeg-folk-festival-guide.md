The Winnipeg Folk Festival is one of Canada's longest-running and most respected music festivals, held every July at Birds Hill Provincial Park just outside Winnipeg, Manitoba. Since 1974 it has grown into a four-day gathering of more than 70 artists across nine stages, with a lineup philosophy that mixes folk tradition with indie rock, alt-country, and global music.

Here is a recap of the 2026 festival, a guide to the Birds Hill experience, and what we know about 2027.

## What Makes Birds Hill Special

Birds Hill Provincial Park sits about 30 minutes northeast of downtown Winnipeg. The festival takes over a huge prairie meadow ringed by aspen forest, and the setting shapes everything about the experience:

- **The tarp run.** Each morning, gates open and attendees sprint (politely, this is Manitoba) to claim main stage tarp spots for the evening concerts. It is a beloved tradition with its own etiquette.
- **Daytime workshops.** Smaller stages host collaborative sessions where artists from different bands perform together. Many longtime attendees consider these the heart of the festival.
- **The campground is its own festival.** The adjacent festival campground is famous for all-night song circles, decorated camps, and a community that returns year after year.
- **Prairie skies.** Sunsets over the main stage meadow are spectacular, and so are the mosquitoes. Bring repellent, sunscreen, and a refillable water bottle.

## Tickets and Camping

The festival offers single-day and full-festival passes, with discounted youth pricing and free admission for young children. Festival camping passes are separate and consistently sell out well before the event, often within days of going on sale in winter.

### Who headlined the Winnipeg Folk Festival in 2026?

### Where is the Winnipeg Folk Festival held?

At Birds Hill Provincial Park, about 30 minutes northeast of downtown Winnipeg, Manitoba. The festival provides shuttle service from the city, and a park vehicle permit is required if you drive.

### Is the Winnipeg Folk Festival family-friendly?

Very. There is a dedicated family area, programming for kids, and free entry for young children. The daytime atmosphere is one of the most relaxed of any major festival.
