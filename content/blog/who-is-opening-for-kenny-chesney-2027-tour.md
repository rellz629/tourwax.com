**[Eric Church](/artists/eric-church), [Sheryl Crow](/artists/sheryl-crow), and [Greylan James](/artists/greylan-james) open every date on [Kenny Chesney](/artists/kenny-chesney)'s Original Vibe Room Tour 2027.** There is exactly one exception: at Highmark Stadium in Buffalo on June 19, **[Old Dominion](/artists/old-dominion) plays in Sheryl Crow's slot**, alongside Church and James.

Chesney announced the 19-date run on **Tuesday, September 15, 2026**, and the support lineup was confirmed in the tour announcement itself rather than trickling out city by city. That makes this one of the simpler opener questions in country music right now: it is the same three acts, in the same order, on 18 of 19 nights. General on-sale is **Friday, September 25, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time** through Ticketmaster.

## The Openers

**Eric Church** is the closest thing to a co-headliner on this bill. He and Chesney have shared stages since Church opened arena dates for him early in his career, and Church spent 2026 on his own [Free the Machine Tour](/blog/who-is-opening-for-eric-church-2026-tour) behind *Evangeline vs. the Machine*. Expect a full set rather than a courtesy half hour.

**Sheryl Crow** is the outlier in the best sense. She is not a country artist, she was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2023, and she has toured with Chesney before. Her catalog is built for a stadium at golden hour, which is roughly when she will be playing.

**Greylan James** opens the show. He is from East Tennessee, the same corner of the state Chesney comes from, and he has spent most of his career writing for other people: cuts with Morgan Wallen, Blake Shelton, Darius Rucker, and Chesney himself. He won the ACM Song of the Year award for co-writing Jordan Davis's "Next Thing You Know." This is his first stadium run.

**Old Dominion** appears once, in Buffalo. The Buffalo Bills confirmed the substitution in their own announcement of the show: the bill there reads Chesney, Church, Old Dominion, and James, with no Sheryl Crow.

## Two Buildings That Do Not Exist Yet

Two stops on this run are opening nights for brand new stadiums, which is unusual for a single tour.

**Nissan Stadium in Nashville** is scheduled to finish construction in February 2027, with concerts and major events starting that April. Chesney's July 3 show will be one of its first. He already holds the record for the most headline appearances at the current Nissan Stadium with six.

**Highmark Stadium in Orchard Park** is the Buffalo Bills' replacement venue, and the Bills announced the June 19 date as **the first concert at the new stadium**. That framing is no longer clean: on September 16, the day after Chesney's announcement, Metallica announced a show at the same building on **June 8, 2027**, which falls 11 days earlier. Buffalo outlets have kept describing Chesney's show as the building's first, and neither camp has publicly sorted out the billing, so treat it as unsettled. Either way, the custom Buffalo lineup makes sense: Old Dominion is a bigger regional draw in western New York than the tour's default third slot.

## Why This Tour Exists

Chesney spent two years at Sphere in Las Vegas, moving close to 400,000 tickets, and the Original Vibe Room Tour is his first return to stadiums since. He kept the framing short at the announcement: "It's gonna be an amazing summer. I can't believe we're here again, and I can't wait to get out there and start doing it."

The all-stadium, all-Saturday structure is the same template that made his 2010s runs work. Nineteen dates is a lean itinerary for a stadium act at his scale, and there is no announced second leg.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is opening for Kenny Chesney in 2027?

Eric Church, Sheryl Crow, and Greylan James open all 19 dates, except Buffalo on June 19, where Old Dominion replaces Sheryl Crow.

### Is Eric Church opening or co-headlining?

Church is billed as support, not a co-headliner, but he plays all 19 dates and is the highest-profile act on the bill besides Chesney. Treat his set as a full one.

### Who is opening for Kenny Chesney in Buffalo?

Eric Church, Old Dominion, and Greylan James. Sheryl Crow is not on the June 19 Highmark Stadium bill. The Buffalo Bills confirmed that lineup in their own announcement.

### Does Sheryl Crow play every date?

Every date except Buffalo on June 19. She is on the other 18 shows.

### When do Kenny Chesney 2027 tickets go on sale?

Friday, September 25, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time, through Ticketmaster. The No Shoes Nation and Vibe Room presales ran September 16 and 17 and have closed.

### How many shows are on the Original Vibe Room Tour?

Nineteen, all stadiums, all Saturdays, from April 24 through August 28, 2027. Each city gets exactly one show.

### Is Kenny Chesney playing New York City or Los Angeles?

No. The closest date to New York City is MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey on August 7. There is no Los Angeles date, and no West Coast date at all: Denver on July 17 is the westernmost stop.

### Will more dates be added?

Nothing further has been announced. The tour was presented as a complete 19-date itinerary, and every Saturday between the opening and closing shows is already spoken for.
