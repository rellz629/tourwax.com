Whether you're planning a trip around a concert or just want to know where the live music action is, some cities consistently deliver more shows, better venues, and stronger lineups than others. We dug into our database of thousands of upcoming events to find the best cities for live music in 2026.

## The Major Hubs

These cities are the backbone of the US touring circuit. Nearly every major tour stops here.

### New York City

The capital of live music in America. From [Madison Square Garden](/venues/madison-square-garden) to intimate Brooklyn clubs, NYC offers more shows per night than almost anywhere else. The city hosts everything from arena tours to jazz residencies.

Browse [concerts in New York](/concerts/new-york).

### Los Angeles

LA's venue landscape spans the legendary [Hollywood Bowl](/venues/hollywood-bowl) to the resurgent downtown scene. The city is a must-stop for pop, hip-hop, Latin, and electronic acts.

Browse [concerts in Los Angeles](/concerts/los-angeles).

### Nashville

Music City lives up to its name. Beyond the honky-tonks on Broadway, Nashville hosts major tours at [Bridgestone Arena](/venues/bridgestone-arena) and has become a destination for genres well beyond country.

Browse [concerts in Nashville](/concerts/nashville).

### Chicago

From [Soldier Field](/concerts/chicago) stadium shows to the legendary blues and jazz clubs, Chicago punches above its weight in live music. The city also hosts some of the country's biggest festivals.

Browse [concerts in Chicago](/concerts/chicago).

## Rising Concert Cities

These cities may surprise you with their growing live music scenes.

### Austin, TX

Beyond SXSW and ACL, Austin's year-round live music scene is one of the deepest in the country. The city's venue density means you can catch multiple shows in a single night.

Browse [concerts in Austin](/concerts/austin).

### Atlanta, GA

Atlanta's hip-hop and R&B scene drives a massive concert calendar. From arena tours to the [Tycoon Music Festival](/festivals/tycoon-music-festival-2026-04-04), the city is a touring essential.

Browse [concerts in Atlanta](/concerts/atlanta).

### Denver, CO

Colorado's capital has become a top touring destination, with [Red Rocks Amphitheatre](/venues/red-rocks-amphitheatre-and-park) drawing artists from every genre. The mountain setting doesn't hurt either.

Browse [concerts in Denver](/concerts/denver).

## Hidden Gems

Don't sleep on smaller markets. These cities offer concerts with shorter lines, easier parking, and often cheaper tickets.

### Lubbock, TX

West Texas's hub for live music has a loyal concert-going community. [Browse concerts in Lubbock](/concerts/lubbock) to see what's coming through.

### Pittsburgh, PA

Steel City's venues punch above their weight class, from theater shows to amphitheater tours. The city's central location makes it a natural stop for East Coast and Midwest tours.

Browse [concerts in Pittsburgh](/concerts/pittsburgh).

### San Antonio, TX

A strong touring market with a mix of country, Latin, and rock shows. San Antonio's venues range from intimate to arena-sized.

Browse [concerts in San Antonio](/concerts/san-antonio).

### Orange Beach, AL

The Gulf Coast concert destination. The [Wharf Amphitheater](/venues/the-wharf-amphitheater) has become a must-stop for country and rock tours.

Browse [concerts in Orange Beach](/concerts/orange-beach).
