The Edmonton Folk Music Festival is one of North America's most beloved outdoor music events, and its home at Gallagher Park is a huge part of the reason. Every August, tens of thousands of fans cover the hillside overlooking downtown Edmonton with tarps and low chairs for four days of folk, roots, and world music as the sun sets behind the skyline.

The 46th edition runs August 6 to 9, 2026. Here is everything you need to know about the festival, the park, and how to plan your visit.

## What Is the Edmonton Folk Music Festival?

Launched in 1980, the Edmonton Folk Music Festival (locals just say "Folk Fest") is a four-day festival held every August at Gallagher Park in Edmonton, Alberta. It consistently ranks among the best folk festivals in the world thanks to its natural amphitheater setting, volunteer-driven culture, and adventurous booking that stretches well beyond traditional folk into indie rock, soul, blues, and global sounds.

The festival always lands on the weekend following the August long weekend, which makes planning ahead easy even before official dates are announced.

## Gallagher Park: The Hill That Makes It Special

Gallagher Park sits in Edmonton's river valley in the Cloverdale neighborhood, directly across the North Saskatchewan River from downtown. The main stage sits at the bottom of a natural bowl, so the entire hillside becomes seating with clear sightlines from nearly everywhere. As night falls, the downtown skyline lights up behind the stage: it is one of the most photographed backdrops in Canadian live music.

A few things to know about the site:

- **The hill is steep.** Comfortable shoes matter, and getting up and down between stages counts as real exercise.
- **Tarp culture rules the hill.** Attendees line up early each morning for the tarp run, claiming spots with low-profile tarps. Chairs must be low-backed so they do not block views.
- **Seven stages run during the day.** Side stages host workshops and collaborative sessions where artists from different bands play together, a Folk Fest signature.
- **Ski hill in winter, festival bowl in summer.** The Edmonton Ski Club operates on the same slope the rest of the year.

## Tickets

Ticket options include single-day tickets and full weekend passes, with youth and senior pricing available. Weekend passes typically sell out first, and Saturday is usually the highest-demand single day.

Tickets are sold through the official festival site at edmontonfolkfest.org. If the festival is sold out, resale marketplaces are the fallback, but prices climb fast in the final week. For other shows passing through Alberta, check the current [concert calendar on TourWax](/concerts).

## Tips for First-Timers

- **Bring a tarp, not a blanket.** Tarps shed evening dew and mark your spot during the day.
- **Layer up.** Edmonton August days can hit 28 C while nights drop near 10 C. A warm layer for the headline set is not optional.
- **Arrive by transit or bike if you can.** Parking near Gallagher Park is very limited. The festival runs shuttle and bike lock-up options, and the Muttart or downtown areas are walkable across the river.
- **Explore the side stages.** The workshop sessions, where multiple artists share a stage and trade songs, produce the moments people talk about for years.
- **Cash in on the food row.** The vendor lineup leans heavily local, and the green onion cake is an Edmonton festival tradition.

## FAQ

### Where is Gallagher Park?

Gallagher Park is in Edmonton's river valley at the base of the Cloverdale neighborhood, across the North Saskatchewan River from downtown Edmonton. In winter it is home to the Edmonton Ski Club; every August it hosts the Edmonton Folk Music Festival.

### When is the Edmonton Folk Music Festival?

The festival always takes place the weekend after the August long weekend. The 2026 edition runs August 6 to 9, 2026. Expect the 2027 edition to land on the corresponding weekend in August 2027, with the lineup typically announced in late spring.

### Who is headlining the 2026 Edmonton Folk Music Festival?

The 2026 lineup is led by Of Monsters and Men, Nathaniel Rateliff, St. Paul & The Broken Bones, Thee Sacred Souls, and Cat Power, alongside more than 60 artists across seven stages.

### Can kids attend?

Yes. Folk Fest is famously family-friendly, with a dedicated kids area and reduced youth pricing. Many Edmonton families treat it as an annual tradition.
